Mini Logic Puzzle Answer Key  
(Instructor Only — delete this section before sending it to students) 
 
Aiko — R&D — Chat 
Ben — Purchasing — Email 
Carlos — Manufacturing — Face-to-face 
Dita — Sales — Call 
 
Explanation of Solution 
1. From Clue 3, Ben is in Purchasing. 
2. Clue 7 says the Sales colleague prefers Call. 
3. Clue 6 says Dita is not in R&D, so Dita must be in Sales (since Sales is still open). 
Thus, Dita → Sales → Call. 
4. Clue 2 says the R&D colleague prefers Chat. Clue 1 says Aiko doesn’t work in Sales 
and doesn’t prefer Call, leaving R&D–Chat as Aiko’s match. 
5. The Manufacturing colleague isn’t Aiko (Clue 5) and Sales is already taken, so Carlos 
must be Manufacturing. Clue 4 confirms Carlos prefers Face-to-face. 
6. That leaves Ben, who must therefore use Email (the only remaining style). 
7. The final matches are therefore: Aiko – R&D – Chat; Ben – Purchasing – Email; Carlos – 
Manufacturing – Face-to-face; Dita – Sales – Call.
