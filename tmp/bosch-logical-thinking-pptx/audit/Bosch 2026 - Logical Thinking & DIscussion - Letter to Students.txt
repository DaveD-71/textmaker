Pre-Course Preparation 
Before our first session, please complete the following tasks: 
1. Read your textbook, Logical Speaking. 
a. Read Units 1–5 & 7–8. Check any unknown vocabulary. The main concepts in 
these units will be reviewed in the course. 
b. Do Activity 1A and review your answers. 
c. Be ready to discuss Activity 1B in class. 
2. Solve this Mini Logic Puzzle. 
Four colleagues—Aiko, Ben, Carlos, and Dita—each works in a different division 
(Manufacturing, Purchasing, R&D, Sales) and prefers a different meeting style (Email, 
Chat, Call, Face-to-face).  
Use the clues below to match each person to their division and meeting style. 
a. Aiko does not work in Sales and does not prefer Calls. 
b. The colleague in R&D prefers Chat. 
c. Ben works in Purchasing. 
d. Carlos prefers Face-to-face meetings. 
e. The Manufacturing colleague is not Aiko. 
f. 
Dita is not in R&D. 
g. The Sales colleague prefers Call. 
Write one sentence for each person linking their name, division, and meeting style (e.g., 
“Aiko – Manufacturing – Email”). Bring your answers to class. This will help you begin 
thinking analytically and start applying logical reasoning in English.

how logic can make your communication more effective and persuasive.
