# Introduction

- **Introduction to *Business Meetings: Essentials***

> *Business Meetings: Essentials* provides the basic concept of the tasks and structure of successful business meetings.
>
> The tasks and structure are illustrated in the brief Model Meeting Transcript.

- **How to Use *Business Meetings: Essentials***

1.  Study the tasks and structure of business meetings.

2.  Read the Model Meeting Transcript and take note of the language that is used.

3.  Practice the tasks and structure by reading the Model Meeting Transcript several times. Each student should have the opportunity to practice each part.

4.  Use the transcript as a platform for meeting simulations during the course.

5.  During the simulations, each student has the opportunity to; be the chairperson, present short reports, and participate in Q&A and discussion.

6.  Before the simulations the chairperson needs to write a chairing script suitable for the type of meeting and the topic for discussion.

7.  After some practice, the students should be able to chair the meetings without reading a chairing script.
