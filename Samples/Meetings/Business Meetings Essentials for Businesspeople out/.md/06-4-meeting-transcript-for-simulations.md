# 4: Meeting Transcript for Simulations

- **Model Meeting Transcript for Meeting Simulations**

Use this transcript as a platform for meeting simulations.

Modify and add language to suit the simulation situation.

- **Roles in the model meeting transcript**

  1.  Chair

  2.  Speaker

  3.  Note Taker

  4.  Participants

<th style="text-align: left;"><blockquote>
<p><strong><u>Starting the Meeting</u></strong></p>
<p>Chair Good __________ everyone.</p>
<p>I’d like to start our meeting now.</p>
<p>Thank you all for being here.</p>
<p>As you know, we are going to discuss (and decide about) __________.</p>
<p>We need to report our discussion/decision to __________.</p>
<p>Did you all receive the memo dated __________?</p>
<p>Participants Yes. / Yes, I did.</p>
<p>Chair Good.</p>
<p>Let me just confirm the details in the memo.</p>
<p>We will each make a short report on __________.</p>
<p>After each report we will have time for questions and discussion.</p>
<p>I will be chairing our first report from __________.</p>
<p>__________ will take notes and report to __________.</p>
<p>Are there any questions?</p>
<p>Participants No. / No, not from me</p>
<p>Chair Thank you.</p>
<p>So, let’s listen to the report from __________.</p>
<p>__________ is from the __________ Dept.</p>
<p>__________, if you please.</p>
<p>Speaker Thank you, Mr./Ms __________.</p>
<p>Good __________ everyone.</p>
<p>It’s a pleasure to __________.</p>
<p>…</p>
<p>I think we should __________.</p>
<p>…</p>
<p>And that concludes my report.</p>
<p>Thank you for listening.</p>
<p>And now I will be happy to take your questions or comments.</p>
<p><strong><u>Conducting Q&amp;A and Discussion</u></strong></p>
<p>Chair Thank you <em><u>Speaker’s Name</u></em>.</p>
<p>That was very interesting.</p>
<p>Now we have time for questions. Yes, __________?</p>
<p>Participant 1 Thank you. I’d like to ask about __________.</p>
<p>Speaker __________.</p>
<p>Participant 1 I see. Thank you.</p>
<p>Chair Are there any more questions?</p>
<p>Yes, __________?</p>
<p>Participant 2 I have a question about __________. __________?</p>
<p>Speaker __________.</p>
<p>Participant 2 Thank you.</p>
<p>Chair We have time for one more question.</p>
<p>Participant 3 You said that _________. _________?</p>
<p>Speaker _____________.</p>
<p>Participant 3 I understand.</p>
<p>Chair I’m sorry, that’s all the time we have for questions.</p>
<p>Thank you everyone. Thank you for your report _____________.</p>
<p><em><u>Note Taker’s Name</u></em> could you review the main points, please?</p>
<p>Note Taker Yes. According to my notes <em><u>Speaker’s Name</u></em> said _________. He/She thinks that ___________.</p>
<p>Chair Thank you ________.</p>
<p>Is there anything to add?</p>
<p>Participants No. / No, not from me. / That seems fine.</p>
<p>Chair Good. Thank you ________.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>We’ll take a short break now.</p>
<p>After our break, _________ will chair, our next report from _________ and __________ will take notes.</p>
<p>Thank you everyone.</p>
<p>We’ll start again in ______ minutes.</p>
<p><strong><u>Focusing the Discussion</u></strong></p>
<blockquote>
<p>Chair Now we need to discuss all the proposals and decide which one to accept.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>I would like to hear from everyone.</p>
<p>We’ll begin with ___________. _________, what do you think?</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Participant 1 I think we should _________. <u>I think</u> this is the best proposal <u>because</u> _________________.</p>
<p>Participant 2 That’s very interesting but <u>I think</u> we should __________ <u>because</u> ________.</p>
<p>Participant 3 I think, so too because ____________.</p>
<p>Participant 4 Actually, I don’t think that’s a good idea. <u>I think</u> we should ______________ <u>because</u> ________.</p>
</blockquote>
<p><strong><u>Making a Decision</u></strong></p>
<blockquote>
<p>Chair We need to make a decision now.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>From our discussion of the proposals there are <u>two</u> proposals to consider: _________ and ___________.</p>
<p>Which do you think is the best proposal? We will have a simple vote.</p>
<p>If you think _________ is the best proposal, please raise your hand.</p>
<p>Thank you. That’s <u>number</u>.</p>
<p>And if you think _________ is the best proposal, please raise your hand.</p>
<p>Thank you. That’s <u>number</u> .</p>
<p>So, it’s decided that ___________ is the best proposal.</p>
<p><em><u>Note Taker’s Name</u></em> would you please report the decision to ______?</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Note Taker Yes, I’ll do that.</p>
</blockquote>
<p><strong><u>Closing the meeting</u></strong></p>
<blockquote>
<p>Chair Thank you very much. And thank you everyone for your time today.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Our meeting is adjourned.</p></th>
