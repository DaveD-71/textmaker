# 9 Model Meeting 1: A Staff Meeting

- **Transcript Notes**

Below is the language for the facilitator to conduct a meeting.

- The meeting includes staff from different departments so the language is somewhat formal.

- Compare this to the language in Model Meeting 2: the language is more informal because the participants work together and have regular meetings.

- Notice how the facilitator and speakers combine the courtesies and appreciations with the functional language.

  - **Meeting Background**

Nippon Taiyo Bank is a large international bank based in Tokyo. It has a goal of becoming a full service international corporate bank.

- As the bank expands overseas the staff has become more international. Dealing with diverse ideas and conflict in meetings has become an important issue.

- As part of its staff development program, the training department has asked the staff to report their meeting experiences and awareness of how to deal with conflict.

- **Simulation 1**

Use the Model E-mail 1 and language in Meeting Transcript 1 to have a meeting simulation.

- The students will take turns facilitating a meeting simulation: every student will facilitate.

- The facilitators will follow the six stages and the protocol of meetings.

- Each student will present a short report about cross cultural meetings (about 3 minutes)

- Each report will include responses to the Cultural & Conflict Quiz.

- There will be Q&A and discussion after each report.

- Each meeting is allotted 15 to 20 minutes.
