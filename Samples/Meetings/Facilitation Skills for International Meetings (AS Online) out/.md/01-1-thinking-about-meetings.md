- **Business Meetings**

> Companies have meetings: Meetings of all kinds.

- Meetings are unavoidable because they are an important part of getting the work done.

- There are many different situations and purposes for meetings, however, they can be divided into two types: Successful and Unsuccessful.

> **Questions to consider**

1.  What does an unsuccessful meeting look like?

2.  What does a successful meeting look like?

- **Creating Successful Meetings**

> Since meetings are both important and unavoidable, they need to be successful all the time. The success of the company depends on it.

Where do successful meetings come from?

- Successful business meetings are created through planning, preparation, and skillful management of the meeting itself.

- A skillful facilitator can avoid potential problems and create successful meetings.
