# 3 Taking Care of a Guest

> ***Simple courtesies***

- Business Situation: At Atlas Construction

> This afternoon, Takashi Ikeda goes to Atlas Construction for another meeting with Amanda Jones. When Mr. Ikeda arrives, Ms Jones is busy so she asks one of her staff members, Helen Wong, to meet Mr. Ikeda and take him to the meeting room.

1.  Meeting the guest

> Wong Excuse me, Mr. Ikeda?
>
> Ikeda Yes, that’s right.
>
> Wong Good afternoon. My name is Helen Wong. Ms Jones asked me to meet you.
>
> Ikeda Thank you very much.
>
> Wong Please follow me. The meeting room is this way.

2.  At the meeting room

> Wong This is the room. Please have a seat here. (Gesturing)
>
> Ikeda Thank you.
>
> Wong Ms Jones will be with you in a moment.
>
> Ikeda Thank you.
>
> (Ms Wong leaves the room.)

3.  Apologizing for a delayed meeting

> Wong Excuse me, Mr. Ikeda.
>
> Ikeda Yes?
>
> Wong I’m very sorry but Ms Jones is still in a meeting. She can be with you in about 10 minutes.
>
> Ikeda That’s quite all right. I understand.
>
> Wong Thank you very much for your patience.
>
> Ikeda Not at all.

4.  Room Change

> Wong Excuse me Mr. Ikeda.
>
> Ikeda Yes?
>
> Wong Thank you for waiting. I’m sorry but the meeting room has been moved to the 10th floor. Please follow me, I’ll take you there.
>
> Ikeda Thank you.

- **Business Protocol**

<!-- -->

- **Business cards**

> When taking care of a guest it is not usually necessary to exchange business cards.

- **Small talk**

> The guest has an appointment to meet Ms. Jones, not Ms. Wong. There is no need to engage in small talk in this situation.

- **Expressions for Introductions**

<!-- -->

- I‘ll take you to the meeting room.

- Please follow me.

- Please have a seat.

- The washroom is at the end of the hall on the left.

- I’m very sorry but …

- I’m very sorry to have kept you waiting.

- Thank you for waiting.
