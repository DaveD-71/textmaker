# Problem-Solving & Decision-Making

Problems arise almost every day in all kinds of situations, and range from minor issues to large, life-changing challenges. Learning to deal with problems effectively and in a timely manner is not only essential for business, but for life in general. It is an important skill which, like all skills, can be improved through practice.

When dealing with problems which involve others, effective communication is essential. The goal of this textbook is to help you learn to become better communicators when dealing with problems. Problem solving is a communication skill.

classical decision model

<div class="Definition">

A prescriptive approach to decision-making that assumes decision-makers are logical and rational, and will make decisions in the best interests of the organization.

</div>

administrative model

<div class="Definition">

The administrative model of decision-making argues that decision-makers (1) have incomplete and imperfect information, (2) are constrained by bounded reality and (3) tend to satisfice when making decisions rather than optimizing outcomes.

</div>

bounded rationality

<div class="Definition">

The concept that decision-makers are limited by their values, attitudes, skills and habits.

</div>

satisficing

<div class="Definition">

The tendency to search for alternatives only until one is found that meets some minimum standard of sufficiency.

</div>
