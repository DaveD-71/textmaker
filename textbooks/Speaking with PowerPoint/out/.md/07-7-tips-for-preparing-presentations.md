#  7 Tips for Preparing Presentations

\[\[PAGEBREAK\]\]

> <img src="assets/media/image9.png" style="width:1.16667in;height:0.71458in" />

- **Preparing Your Speech**

Prepare your speech systematically.

1.  **Prepare your speech in English**

> Don’t prepare it in Japanese and then translate it.

- Writing in Japanese and then translating is a waste of time and the results will not be very good.

- Think about your topic in English and write your notes in English.

2.  **Think about why you are speaking**

> What is the purpose of your speech?

- What is your message?

3.  **Think about your audience**

> Who is your audience?

- What do they need to know?

4.  **Write your speech**

> Write in simple clear English.

- Follow the basic structure.

- Use the flag expressions.

- Be logical & persuasive.

5.  **Prepare your PowerPoint slides**

> Follow the design rules.
>
> Check the design by printing or projecting your PowerPoint.

6.  **Practice your presentation (speech + PowerPoint)**

> Practice your presentation.

- Practice delivering your presentation out loud with the delivery skills.

- Make changes and corrections.

- Practice again.

7.  **PowerPoint**

> Handouts: Print copies of your PowerPoint handout.

- Put them in a folder to protect them.

> PowerPoint Slides

- Carry a backup copy on a thumb drive / flash memory stick.

- E-mail it to yourself for back up.

- Take hardcopies for extra backup.

8.  **Speaking Notes & Speech Text**

    - Speaking Notes

- If you write speaking notes on cards, make sure the words are large and easy to read.

  - Speech Text

- Prepare a neat copy of your speech text.

- Make sure your text is easy to read with wide margins on the top, bottom, and sides as in Sample A below. Sample A is much easier to read than Sample B.

- Don’t add last minute changes to your text. You will not be able to read disorganized notes during your presentation.
