- **Model Speech 2: Product Description**

> Below is a product description for an in-company meeting. It includes elements of description, logic, persuasion, and critical thinking.

- **Background to the model speech**

> The speaker is Steve Ridley, the head of a design team at Prestige Motors, a large car manufacturer. In this presentation, Mr. Ridley introduces the new model of the Ventura to the marketing team.

- **Instructions**

> Read and practice the speech. Identify the *structure*, *flag expressions, theme and conclusion* and examples of description, logic, critical thinking, and persuasion. You can use this speech as a model for your own speeches.

<td><p>1. Good afternoon, everyone. I am glad to be here to introduce the all-new Ventura for the 2014 market. As you know, the Ventura has been a very popular family sedan and it has just been redesigned to meet the changing market conditions.</p>
<p>2. Today, my talk will cover three main areas - first, we’ll take a brief look at Ventura’s sales performance; second, I’ll explain the process of redesigning the Ventura; and third, I’ll introduce some of the features of the new Ventura. I’ll be happy to take your questions after my talk.</p>
<p>3. Let me begin with a look at Ventura’s sales performance from 2000 to 2013. The Ventura debuted in the year 2000, with strong sales of 152,300 units in the domestic market. Sales grew steadily each year and peaked in 2003 at 235,500 units. In 2006, sales dropped sharply to 221,800 units so we decided to update the design. With the new design sales recovered dramatically in 2008 and peaked in 2010 at 249,900 units. By 2012, sales had dropped to 234,100 units so we decided to remodel the Venture again for the 2014 market.</p>
<p>4. Now, let me explain how we redesigned the Ventura. First, we carefully read customer feedback; then we studied Ventura’s safety record; and finally we examined the environmental standards.</p>
<p>5. With these three areas in mind: 1. design; 2. safety; and 3. environmental standards, we looked for ways to improve the Ventura. The new Ventura is the result of this process.</p>
<p>6. Now, let’s look at Ventura’s features one by one.</p>
<p>7. To begin with, the new Ventura has unequalled design and comfort. Its sporty design, powerful 3-liter engine, and spacious interior make the new Ventura fun to drive.</p>
<p>8. The second feature is safety. Ventura’s improved safety features include a stronger frame; an advanced Collision Avoidance System that warns about dangers; and an Integrated Crash Protection System of upgraded airbags and seat belts that provides full protection. The Ventura is a very safe car to drive.</p>
<p>9. Finally, the Ventura is environmentally responsible. With its efficient hybrid engine and ultra-low emissions, it far exceeds the government’s targets. In addition, 95% of the car is made of recyclable material. So, totally, the Ventura is an environmentally responsible car.</p>
<p>10. In conclusion, I would like to say that because of these features - its design, safety, and environmental responsibility - we are confident the new Ventura will recover its position as a leading high performance family sedan and exceed its past sales performance.</p>
<p>11. Thank you for listening.</p>
<p>Now, I’ll be happy to answer your questions. …</p>
<p>Yes, Mr. Watson?</p></td>
