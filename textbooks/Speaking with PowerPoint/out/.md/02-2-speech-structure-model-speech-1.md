- **Speeches have three parts**

> All speeches have the same three-part structure. Each part of the structure is important. They each have a specific purpose and contribute to the success of the speech.

- **Exercise**: Write your self-introductory speech. Follow the same structure and use the same expressions as the model.

> You can expand your introduction with some of the points below:

- Why I joined my company.

- The work I have done and the work I am currently doing.

- The work I have enjoyed the most and why.

- What I would like to do in the future and why.

> **Note about Vocabulary for Work**

- Don’t say, “I’m <u>in charge of</u>…” unless you are the boss. Say, “I t<u>ake care of</u>…”

- Don’t say, “I <u>belong to</u> the general affairs department.” Say, “I <u>work in</u> the general affairs department.”
