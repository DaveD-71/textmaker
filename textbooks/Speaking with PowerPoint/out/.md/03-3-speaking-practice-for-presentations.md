- **<u>7 Elements for Speaking Clearly</u>**

In addition to good pronunciation and overcoming katakana, English has other elements for clear speaking. These elements make your words easier to understand and give meaning to your words. Without the elements it can be difficult to understand the speaker’s meaning.

The elements are common to native speakers of English; however, how they are used varies from speaker to speaker. The samples below are just one way to apply the elements. Practice out loud and you will learn how to use the elements to speak more clearly.

> **The Elements Voice Symbols**

1.  Speak slowly. Say each word carefully.

2.  Lengthen some vowels slightly: <u>a</u>, <u>e</u>, <u>i</u>, <u>o</u>, <u>u</u>

3.  Speak in groups of words & pause slightly between them: **/** (short pause)

4.  Pause between sentences: **//** (medium pause)

5.  Pause between paragraphs: **///** (longer pause)

6.  Stress key words and pause before & after them: Stress key words

7.  Speak with intonation –rising, falling & flat:

- **<u>The 7 Elements: Samples & Application</u>**

**Samples:** Practice each of the 7 Elements for Speaking Clearly by saying the passage carefully out loud at least three times: the more the better.

**Application:** After practicing the samples below, apply each element to your own speech text and practice.

1.  **Speak slowly. Say each word carefully.**

> Good morning everyone. It’s a pleasure to be here. Let me tell you a little about myself.
>
> My name is Claus Meyer. I’m from Stuttgart, Germany. I’ve been working for IMC for fifteen years.

2.  **Lengthen some vowels slightly for clarity.**

> G**<u>oo</u>**d m**<u>o</u>**rning **<u>e</u>**veryone. It’s a pl**<u>ea</u>**sure to b**<u>e</u>** here. L**<u>e</u>**t me t**<u>e</u>**ll you a little about m**<u>y</u>**self.
>
> M**<u>y</u>** n**<u>a</u>**me is Cl**<u>au</u>**s M**<u>e</u>**yer. **<u>I</u>**’m from St**<u>u</u>**ttgart, G**<u>e</u>**rmany. **<u>I</u>**’ve been w<u>o</u>rking for **<u>I</u>** **<u>M</u>** **<u>C</u>** for fift**<u>ee</u>**n years.

3.  **Speak / in groups of words / & / pause slightly / between the groups.**

4.  **Pause between sentences. //**

5.  **Pause between paragraphs. ///**

> Good morning / everyone. / It’s a / pleasure / to be here. // Let me tell you / a little / about myself. ///
>
> My name is / Claus / Meyer. I’m from / Stuttgart, / Germany. // \[\[LINEBREAK\]\]I’ve been working for / I/M/C / for / fifteen / years. //

6.  **Stress key words & pause before & after key words**

> Good morning everyone. It’s a pleasure to be here. Let me tell you a little about myself.
>
> My name is Claus Meyer. I’m from Stuttgart, Germany. I’ve been working for I M C for fifteen years.

7.  **Speak with intonation – the voice rising and falling.**

> Good morning everyone. It’s a pleasure to be here. Let me tell you
>
> a little about myself.
>
> My name is Claus Meyer. I’m from Stuttgart, Germany. I’ve been
>
> working for I/M/C for fifteen years.
