# 10 Special Announcements 

> *Changes, appreciations, & condolences*

- **Making special announcements**

> Sometimes it is necessary for the chairperson to make special announcement to explain changes to the program or express appreciation or condolences. Below are some sample announcements.

- **Change of chair**

> I need to announce a change of chair for this session. The agenda indicates that Anita Rawlins was to chair the session. However …

1.  Due to a last minute change to her schedule she is not able to attend the conference.

2.  I’m sorry to say that due to illness she is not able to attend the conference. We wish her a speedy recovery.

> In her place, Sindra Anwar will chair the session. We appreciate Ms Anwar stepping in at the last minute.

- **Expressing appreciations to staff at the close of the conference**

> In closing, I would like to express our appreciation to all the staff members who worked so hard behind the scenes organizing and managing this conference. And also special thanks to the convention center staff and management who provided us with such kind and efficient hospitality. It was through their efforts that we, the participants, were able to focus on the issues at hand for the past three days. Let me ask you all to join me now in expressing our appreciation with a round of applause.

- **Condolences for illness**

> I am sorry to say that our respected colleague, Rahine Carter will not be able to join us for this conference. She has recently been ill but fortunately she is recovering. I’m sure you join me in wishing her all the best for a full recovery.

- **Condolences for passing**

> I am very sad to have to inform you of the recent passing of our good friend and colleague Randal Whitehead. Until recently he was a regular and active participant in these conferences and we will miss him very much. I know you will join me in extending our sincere condolences to his wife and family.
