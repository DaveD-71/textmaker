- **Meetings & Conferences**

> Meetings and conferences are gatherings of people for the purpose of achieving specific goals. In many ways meetings are social events: this must be kept in mind to make them successful. In order to achieve their specific goals, meetings and conferences need to be well managed.

- The first goal in a meeting is to create rapport: an atmosphere of communication in which people are willing to speak and to listen.

- When rapport is established the meeting can go to its next goal: creating understanding.

- From understanding, the next goal can be achieved: agreement.

- Finally, the ultimate goal of the meeting can be achieved: making a decision.

> When we understand and use the dynamics of meetings – the tasks, structure, and language – we can create successful meetings.

- **Tasks in a Meeting**

> The first thing to understand about managing a meeting is that the people in the meeting have different tasks and responsibilities. Each task contributes to the success of the meeting.
>
> The five tasks include:

1.  Chairing

2.  Speaking / Topic Leader

3.  Participating

4.  Taking notes

5.  Time keeping

- **Meetings vs. Conferences**

> Although meetings follow the same procedures and have the same tasks, they vary in formality and style according to:

- who is attending,

- how many are attending, and

- the purpose of the meeting.

> The common factor in all successful meetings is the chairperson: successful meetings have effective chairpeople.
