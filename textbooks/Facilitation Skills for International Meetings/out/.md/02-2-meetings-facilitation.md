# 2 Meetings & Facilitation

> <img src="assets/media/image4.png" style="width:2.25in;height:1.48889in" />

A meeting is a dynamic process.

Gathering resources towards a specific goal.

> **The Basics**

- **What is a meeting?**

> A meeting is a gathering of particular people, at a particular time, for a set duration, for the purpose of achieving a goal that will improve a company’s operations, products, or services in order to increase profitability.

- **Facilitation: What does it mean?**

> Facilitation means to make a process easier and more successful.

- **Chairperson Vs Facilitator– What’s the difference?**

> Successful meetings require a manager who can guide the step by step process of the meeting from beginning to end so the participants can achieve the purpose of the meeting.

- The meeting manager can be called the chairperson or facilitator. However, there is a difference.

- A chairperson is a participating manager. The chairperson can participate in the discussion and the decision making.

- A facilitator is a neutral manager. The facilitator *does not* participate in the discussion or decisions.
