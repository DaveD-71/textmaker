# 10 Model Meeting 2: A Planning Meeting

- **Meeting Background**

> The New Products Marketing team at Nippon Taiyo Bank is tasked with identifying, developing, and launching new financial products and services. The decision to develop a project is made by consensus within the team but the department head, Takaya Koike, gives the final approval after examining the business model. The team is meeting to discuss a proposal for a new service: micro-financing.
>
> The team members are from five different countries:

1.  Yuki Sato – Japan - Facilitator

2.  Belinda Pradesh – India – Topic leader

3.  Bob Axley – U.S

4.  Dieter Hinkle - Germany

5.  Terry Lin – Hong Kong

6.  Mai Hoshino – Japan – Note Taker

<p>Every month, overseas workers all over the world remit billions of dollars to their home countries. This is a huge market that we could develop here and overseas.</p>
<p>The first step is to offer foreign workers in this country a special account that includes a low cost remittance service to accounts in their home countries. This would simplify the transfer for the workers and increase the number of depositors for us. It would also give us tie-ups with banks in overseas markets.</p>
<p>The next step would be to offer micro-financing to depositors with good track records. Their families could use the loans to develop their farms or businesses. After that we could offer micro-financing to communities for infrastructure projects such as water, sanitation, and electrification.</p>

- **  
  Simulation 2**

\[\[PAGEBREAK\]\]

Use Model E-mail 2 and the language in Meeting Transcript 2 to have a meeting simulation.

- The students will take turns facilitating a meeting simulation: every student will facilitate.

- The facilitators will follow the six stages and the protocol of meetings.

- Each student will make a short presentation to make a proposal (about 3 minutes).

- The proposal should be based on the student’s own work experience.

- Prepare the presentation with the Project Proposal form.

- Using Belinda Pradesh’s presentation as a model.

- There will be Q&A and discussion after each report.

- Each meeting is allotted 15 to 20 minutes.

Creating a Proposal

\[\[PAGEBREAK\]\]

Prepare a brief report on a proposal for your work.

Report length: Brief - About 3 minutes
