# 2: Model Speech 1: Self Introduction

- **Model Self-Introductory Speech**

> Below is a model self-introductory speech. It is very short however, it follows the basic speech structure of introduction, body, and conclusion. Read the speech carefully.
>
> Background: Hiroshi Tanaka works for the Japan Marine Security Agency in Tokyo. Below is the self-introductory speech he gave at a training program.

<td><p><strong>1. <u>Introduction</u></strong></p>

<li><p>Greetings &amp; courtesies</p></li>

<td><blockquote>
<p>Thank you, Mr. / Ms (<em>Chairperson’s name</em>). Good afternoon everyone. It’s a pleasure to be here. As Mr. / Ms (<em>Chairperson’s name</em>) said, my name is <em><u>Hiroshi Tanaka</u>.</em> I’d like to tell you a little about myself and my work at the Japan Marine Security Agency.</p>
</blockquote></td>

<tr>
<td><p><strong>2. <u>Body</u></strong></p>
<ul>
<li><p>My background, interests &amp; comments</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><blockquote>
<p>I was born in Hokkaido but I grew up and went to school in Kyoto. In high school I liked world history and geography and belonged to the International Club. I also enjoyed playing sports on the school teams. I really liked playing volleyball. Our team was quite good and we won several local tournaments.</p>
</blockquote></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><ul>
<li><p>My work at JMSA</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><blockquote>
<p>At university I studied international affairs and had an interest in security. I found it very interesting and wanted to do some kind of international work. My advisor at university suggested I apply to Japan Marine Security Agency. I passed the exams and the interviews and was offered a job. I was very happy.</p>
</blockquote></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><ul>
<li><p>General statement</p></li>
<li><p>My work at JMSA</p></li>
<li><p>Thinking about my future</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><blockquote>
<p>So far I have worked in two bureaus at JMSA. First, I worked in the General Affairs Bureau and now I am working in the Research Bureau. My current work is very interesting and I am really enjoying it. I am looking forward to attending conferences overseas and possibly working in a foreign country in a couple of years.</p>
</blockquote></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>3. <u>Conclusion</u></strong></p>
<ul>
<li><p>Courtesies</p></li>
<li><p>Closing courtesy</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><blockquote>
<p>I am very happy to be here today and I am glad to be working with you all.</p>
<p>Thank you very much.</p>
</blockquote></td>
