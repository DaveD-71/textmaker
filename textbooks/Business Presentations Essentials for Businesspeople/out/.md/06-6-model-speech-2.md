- **Company Introduction**

> Hiroshi Tanaka had to make a short speech to introduce Nippon Taiyo Bank. He described a little about the history and activities of NTB. Below is his speech.

<p>The Nippon Taiyo Bank is a large integrated bank based in Tokyo. It was created in 2004 with the merger of two banks. The two banks were the Marunouchi Bank and the Musashi Bank. The Marunouchi Bank was a mid-sized domestic retail bank which focused on providing banking services to individual customers and small businesses. The Musashi Bank was a large corporate bank which provided financial services to mid-sized and large corporate clients domestically and internationally.</p>
</blockquote></td>

<p>The Nippon Taiyo Bank is a well-capitalized institution with retail and corporate branches throughout the country. Currently, we have 257 branches providing a full range of financial services to individuals as well as small and medium sized businesses. Our large corporate clients are taken care of by our head office and our four regional offices. In addition, Nippon Taiyo has seven overseas branches in Asia, North America, and Europe providing financial and investment services to our corporate clients.</p>
<p>NTB’s corporate clients come from all sectors of the economy. Our traditional strength has been providing asset finance, project finance and M&amp;A advisory services to the manufacturing and heavy industry sectors however, in recent years we have also been growing in the IT, communication, and service sectors.</p>
