<p><strong>1. Decide Topics (3 minutes)</strong></p>
</blockquote></td>
<td style="text-align: center;"><strong>Time</strong></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><ol type="1">
<li><p><strong>Brainstorm Topics</strong><br />
Have all the students brainstorm speech topics and write one topic on a piece of paper.</p></li>
</ol></td>
<td style="text-align: center;"><blockquote>
<p>1 min.</p>
</blockquote></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><ol start="2" type="1">
<li><p><strong>Check</strong><br />
Collect and check the topics and make corrections if necessary.</p></li>
</ol></td>
<td style="text-align: center;"><blockquote>
<p>1 min.</p>
</blockquote></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><ol start="3" type="1">
<li><p><strong>Draw</strong><br />
Each student draws a topic at random - (not their own topic).</p></li>
</ol></td>
<td style="text-align: center;"><blockquote>
<p>1 min.</p>
</blockquote></td>
</tr>

<p><strong>2. Prepare &amp; Practice (10 minutes)</strong></p>
</blockquote></td>
<td style="text-align: center;"><strong>Time</strong></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><ol type="1">
<li><p><strong>Brainstorm</strong><br />
The students brainstorm their topics and write their notes with coaching.</p></li>
</ol></td>
<td style="text-align: center;"><blockquote>
<p>2 min.</p>
</blockquote></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><ol start="2" type="1">
<li><p><strong>Organize with a Logic Tree</strong><br />
The students organize their brainstorming notes into a logic tree.</p></li>
</ol></td>
<td style="text-align: center;"><blockquote>
<p>3 min.</p>
</blockquote></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><ol start="3" type="1">
<li><p><strong>Practice</strong><br />
The students stand to review and practice their presentation.</p></li>
</ol></td>
<td style="text-align: center;"><blockquote>
<p>5 min.</p>
</blockquote></td>
</tr>

<p><strong>3. Set Up (2 minutes)</strong></p>
</blockquote></td>
<td style="text-align: center;"><strong>Time</strong></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><ol type="1">
<li><p><strong>Presentation Set-up</strong><br />
The students write their names on the speech topic sheets. The instructor collects them.</p></li>
</ol>
<blockquote>
<p>- The students put their speech notes away while they listen to each presentation and take notes.</p>
</blockquote></td>
<td style="text-align: center;"><blockquote>
<p>1 min.</p>
</blockquote></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><ol start="2" type="1">
<li><p><strong>Chairing</strong><br />
The first chairperson volunteers, draws a topic at random, and introduces the first speaker.</p></li>
</ol>
<blockquote>
<p>Note: The first speaker becomes the second chairperson. The first chair is the last speaker.</p>
</blockquote></td>
<td style="text-align: center;"><blockquote>
<p>1 min.</p>
</blockquote></td>
</tr>
