#  8: Language for Chairing 

\[\[PAGEBREAK\]\]

- **How to introduce the speaker**

> Practice the expressions below and then introduce a speaker.

- **Chairperson: Introducing the speaker**

> Good afternoon, everyone. It’s my pleasure to introduce our first / next / last speaker.
>
> His/Her name is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
>
> Please give M \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ a warm welcome.
>
> M \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, if you please.

- **Speaker: Before speaking**

> Thank you for the introduction, M \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_. Good afternoon everyone.

- **Chairperson: After the presentation**

> Thank you M \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_. That was very interesting. / Thank you for your interesting speech M \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
>
> Now we have time for some questions.

- We have time for one more question.

- I’m sorry that’s all the time we have for questions.

- If there are no more questions we will close the discussion.

> Thank you for your questions everyone. And thank you again, M \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, for your interesting presentation.

- **Chairperson: Asking the instructor for feedback**

> Now I’d like to ask M (Teacher’s name) for some feedback. M \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, if you please.
>
> Thank you M \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_. That was very useful feedback.

- **Chairperson: Transferring to the next chair**

> Now, I would like to ask M \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ to introduce the next speaker. M \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, if you please.

- **The Last Chairperson: Closing the session**

> And that concludes our presentations. Thank you everyone.
