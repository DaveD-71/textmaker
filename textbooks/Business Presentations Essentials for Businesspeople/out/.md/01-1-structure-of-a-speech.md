\[\[PAGEBREAK\]\]

<td style="text-align: left;"><blockquote>
<p>This is the main part of your speech.</p>

<li><p>Keep your audience interested!</p></li>
</ul></td>

</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"></td>
<td style="text-align: left;"><blockquote>
<p>This is the wrap up – make a strong finish.</p>
</blockquote>
<ul>
<li><p>Review your theme and your main points.</p></li>
<li><p>Do not introduce any new ideas or new information.</p></li>
</ul>
<blockquote>
<p>Deliver your message clearly: it is a vital part of your conclusion.</p>
</blockquote>
<ul>
<li><p>Clearly express your opinion or state what action is needed.</p></li>
<li><p>Leave your audience thinking about your message!</p></li>
