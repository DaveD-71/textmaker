# 8 Model Speech 2: Product Description

- **Product Description**

Below is a product report for an in-company meeting. It follows the same pattern as the self-introduction speech but it has more detail.

- Read and practice the speech. Pay attention to the *structure*, *flag expressions, theme and conclusion*. You can use this speech as a model for your own speeches.

<!-- -->

- **Background**

Prestige Motors is a large car manufacturer. One of their most popular cars is the Ventura. Steve Ridley is a team leader in the design and development department. This afternoon, Mr. Ridley is going to meet the marketing division to introduce a new model of the Ventura. The text of his speech is below.

<td style="text-align: left;"><p>1. Good afternoon, everyone. I am glad to be here to introduce the all-new Ventura for next year’s market. As you know, the Ventura has been a very popular family sedan and it has just been redesigned to meet the changing market conditions.</p>
<p>2. Today, my talk will cover three main areas - first, we’ll take a brief look at Ventura’s year-to-year sales performance; second, I’ll explain the process of redesigning the Ventura; and third, I’ll introduce some of the features of the new Ventura. I’ll be happy to take your questions after my talk.</p>
<p>3. Let me begin with a look at Ventura’s sales performance. As you know, the Ventura debuted in 2001 with strong sales of 99,000 units in the domestic and overseas markets. Sales grew steadily each year and peaked in 2005 at 149,000 units. Sales remained steady until 2007. In 2009, sales dropped, so we decided to redesign the Ventura.</p>
<p>4. Now, let me explain how we redesigned the Ventura. First, we carefully read the customer feedback; then we studied Ventura’s safety record; and finally we examined the environmental standards.</p>
<p>5. With these three areas in mind: 1. design; 2. safety; and 3. environmental standards, we looked for ways to improve the Ventura. The new Ventura is the result of this process.</p>
<p>6. Now, let’s look at the features one by one.</p>
<p>7. To begin with, the new Ventura has unequalled design and comfort. Its sporty design, powerful 3-liter engine, and spacious interior make the new Ventura fun to drive.</p>
<p>8. The second feature is safety. The improved safety features include a stronger frame; a danger alert system; and a crash protection system of airbags and seat belts for full protection. The Ventura is a very safe car to drive.</p>
<p>9. Finally, the Ventura is environmentally responsible. It has a hybrid engine with very low emissions. In addition, 95% of the car can be recycled. So, totally, the Ventura is an environmentally responsible car.</p>
<p>10. In conclusion, I would like to say that because of these features - its design, safety, and environmental responsibility - we are confident the new Ventura will recover its position as a leading high performance family sedan and exceed its past sales performance.</p>
<p>11. Thank you for listening. Now, I’ll be happy to answer your questions. … Yes, Mr. Watson?</p></td>

- **Exercise 1**

Read the model speech aloud: study its structure and language.

- Find the three parts of the speech and label them by name.

- Underline the topic sentence and mark it with a *T*.

- Underline the main points.

- Underline the flag expressions.

- Mark the greetings and courtesies with *G.*

- Underline the concluding statements in each part and for the whole speech and mark them with a *C*.

- What is the theme?

- What is the message?
