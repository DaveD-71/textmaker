- **Questions and Answers**

> Questions from the audience are an essential part of a presentation. They mean the presentation was successful.

- If the people in your audience ask questions, it means they were listening to your presentation.

- People in the audience should always be prepared to ask questions.

- **How to ask questions**

<!-- -->

- Address your question to the person by name.

- Always refer specifically to what the other speaker said.\[\[LINEBREAK\]\]For example: *Mr. Smith, you said…. Could you tell me more about this?*

<!-- -->

- **How to respond to questions**

<!-- -->

- Respond quickly.

- If you don’t understand the question, don’t try to answer it and don’t wait: immediately ask the person to repeat or explain the question.

- If you need time to think about the answer, tell the audience you are thinking. Don’t make them wait in silence.

- If you can’t answer a question, tell the audience you can’t answer.

<!-- -->

- **Expressions for responding to questions**

1.  *Thank you for the question.*

2.  *That’s an interesting question.*

3.  *I’m sorry, I don’t understand your question.*

4.  *I’m sorry, could you repeat your question, please?*

5.  *I’m sorry, could you rephrase your question, please?*

6.  *That’s a difficult question. Let me think about it for a moment.*

7.  *Does that answer your question?*

8.  *That’s very difficult to explain. Could I explain after the meeting?*

9.  *I’m sorry. I don’t have any information about that, but if you give me your e-mail address, I can send you some information later.*

- **Responding to the Answer**

> It is important to tell the speaker when you understand the answer.

Thank you. I understand.
