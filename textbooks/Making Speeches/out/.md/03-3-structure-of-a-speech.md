- **A Speech Needs One theme.**

> All information and ideas in the speech comes from the theme.
>
> Your theme must be specific. You can create your theme by asking the “Wh Questions”. For example:

**Your theme:** *“Recent trends and the future outlook of ABC’s overseas markets.”*

- **A Speech Needs a Clear Message**

> The message is an essential part of your speech.
>
> Your message answers the Why question: Why am I speaking about this theme?

- What is my purpose?

> Your message needs to be clear and specific. It will often include a proposal: What do you want your audience to do?

- Messages often include words such as because, therefore, and, I think…

> Vague general statements without an opinion or specific proposal are not messages.

- Without a clear message there is no point in giving the speech.
