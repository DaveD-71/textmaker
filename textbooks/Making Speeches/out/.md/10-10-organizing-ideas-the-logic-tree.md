# 10 Organizing Ideas: The Logic Tree

- **What is a Logic Tree?**

> When you make notes for your speech, use a logic tree.
>
> A Logic Tree is like a chart. It is an easy way to organize and remember your ideas because it has a place for each part of your speech: the theme, main points, details for each point, and your message.

- **Why is your speech like a tree?**

> Can you name the parts of the tree?

- <img src="assets/media/image15.png" style="width:1.16667in;height:0.71458in" />**Tips for Preparing Speeches**

1.  **Prepare your speech in English.**

> Don’t prepare it in Japanese and then translate it.

- Writing in Japanese and then translating is a waste of time and the results will not be very good.

- Think about your topic in English and write your notes in English.

2.  **Think about why you are speaking.**

> What is the purpose of your speech?

- What is your message? (Page 5)

3.  **Think about your audience.**

> Who is your audience?

- What do they need to know?

4.  **Gather your ideas by brainstorming.** (Page 18)

> Select the ideas that explain and illustrate your message.

- Think about what questions the audience might ask. Be prepared to answer them.

5.  **Organize your ideas with a logic tree.** (Page 23)

6.  **Write your presentation:**

> Write in simple clear English.

- Follow the basic structure. (Page 4)

- Use the flag expressions. (Page 6)

- Be persuasive. (Page 27)

7.  **Practice your presentation.**

> Practice out loud: speaking clearly (page 10) with the delivery skills. (Page 12)

- Make changes and corrections as you practice.

8.  **Prepare a neat copy of your notes or speech that you can read easily.**

> Type or write your text with wide margins on the top, bottom, and sides as in Sample A below.

- Sample A is much easier to read than Sample B.

> Don’t add last minute changes to your text.

- You will not be able to read disorganized notes.
