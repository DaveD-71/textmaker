- **What is Brainstorming?**

Brainstorming is a way of creating and gathering ideas.

Brainstorming is a way of looking at things in a new way.

- **The Rules for Brainstorming**

</blockquote></td>

<tr>
<td style="text-align: center;"><ol type="1">
<li><p><strong>Everyone must contribute.</strong></p></li>
</ol></td>
</tr>

<td style="text-align: center;"><ol start="2" type="1">
<li><p><strong>Set a time limit: No more than 5 minutes at one time.</strong></p></li>

<li><p><strong>Work quickly.</strong></p></li>
</ol></td>

</ol></td>
</tr>

<td style="text-align: center;"><ol start="5" type="1">
<li><p><strong>Do not discuss any ideas.</strong></p></li>
</ol></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: center;"><ol start="6" type="1">
<li><p><strong>Do not evaluate any ideas.</strong></p></li>
</ol></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: center;"><ol start="7" type="1">
<li><p><strong>Write down every idea where everyone can see it.</strong></p></li>
</ol></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: center;"><ol start="8" type="1">
<li><p><strong>Write in point form; do not write sentences.</strong></p></li>
</ol></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: center;"><ol start="9" type="1">

</ol></td>

> **Remember**: Just like a real storm, brainstorming isn’t neat and tidy.
>
> Brainstorming is messy. Ideas come at different times and in an unexpected order.

> When Steve Ridley prepared his speech about the new Ventura for the marketing division, the first thing he did was brainstorm to gather his ideas. He had done group brainstorming before, so he followed the same procedure even though he was working by himself.
