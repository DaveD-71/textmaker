# 17 Team Presentations

- **Structure & Language of Team Presentations**

> **Structure**

1.  Teams are made up of 2 or 3 students.

2.  Team members must participate equally in the preparation and presentation.

3.  Team members divide the presentation into different parts: For instance - One member presents the introduction and one part of the body and the other member presents the other part of the body and the conclusion.

4.  The first speaker should introduce the structure of the presentation - Who is doing what?

5.  The closing speaker should mention the other speaker.

6.  Speakers should use the pronouns “we” and “our” as well as “I” and “my”. For example –

- <u>We</u> have three reasons for <u>our</u> position.

- <u>I</u> will explain <u>our</u> first two.

- <u>My</u> colleague Mr. Tanaka will explain <u>our</u> third point.

- Let <u>me</u> begin with <u>our</u> first point.

> **Vocabulary and Expressions**

- I am the first speaker for our team. My teammates are Hiro Tanaka and Mari Watanabe.

- I will explain the problem. Mr. Tanaka will explain the costs and Ms Watanabe will present our proposal to solve the problem.

- Now, my colleague Mr. Tanaka will…

- Thank you, Mr. Tomita.

- Mr. Tomita explained the problem. I will now explain the costs of the problem.

- So, in conclusion, my colleagues and I…

- In conclusion, let me review our arguments.

- On behalf of my colleagues, Mr. Tanaka and Ms Watanabe, thank you for listening to our presentation.
