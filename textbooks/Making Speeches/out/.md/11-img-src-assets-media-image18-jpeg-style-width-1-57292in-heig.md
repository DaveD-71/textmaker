- **Five Elements of Persuasion**

> We can identify five different elements of persuasion:

1.  **Message** Your speech has a clear purpose and message.

> Why are you speaking? What do you want your audience to know and understand?

2.  **Logic** Your speech has a clear structure and your message is illustrated with -

- Details, names, numbers, and statistics.

- Examples.

- Stories the audience will understand.

- Your personal experience.

3.  **Language** You use language appropriate for the topic and your audience.

4.  **Delivery** You use effective delivery skills with appropriate:

- posture, gestures, eye contact, and voice.

5.  **Confidence** You believe that what you are saying is important and speak with enthusiasm.
