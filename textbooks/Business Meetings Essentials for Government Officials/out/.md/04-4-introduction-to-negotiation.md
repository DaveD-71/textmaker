# 4: Introduction to Negotiation 

The foundation of successful negotiation

- **What is negotiation?**

> Basically, negotiation is a <u>meeting</u> between two parties to <u>create</u> <u>understanding</u> and an <u>agreement</u> to <u>solve a problem</u>.
>
> The key words in that statement are:

1.  Meeting: Negotiation is communication and needs to go through the same patterns and processes of a meeting.

2.  Create: The two parties ultimately must work together in a creative process. They must create two things that did not exist before.

3.  Understanding: The first thing to be created is understanding. The two parties need to understand each other. Both parties must provide appropriate information at the right time and ask questions for clarification at the right time.

4.  Agreement: Agreement is the second thing to be created – after understanding. If true understanding does not exist between the two parties they cannot create an agreement that will solve the problem.

5.  Solve a problem: When a problem is truly solved, both parties can accept the terms and conditions. If one of the parties does not feel satisfied, the “agreement” will not survive.

> Agreement does not mean that both parties achieved everything they want: that is not possible. To achieve agreement compromises must be carefully considered, offered and exchanged.

- **Succeeding in negotiation**

> Successful negotiators have 7 common characteristics.
>
> Successful negotiators are:

1.  Well-informed about the situation.

2.  Understand their true interests and can explain them.

3.  Able to make and consider proposals.

4.  Focused on the problem and not distracted by their counterparty as a “friend” or an “enemy”.

5.  Systematic about the process of problem solving and conducting meetings every time.

6.  Able to listen to their counterparty.

7.  Able to speak clearly to the point with logic and reasoning.

- **The basics of negotiation**

> These are the necessary basics of negotiation in any language. When you have developed these basics, you are on your way to becoming a successful negotiator.
