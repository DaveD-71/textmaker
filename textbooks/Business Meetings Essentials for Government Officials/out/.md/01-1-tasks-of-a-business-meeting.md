# 1: Tasks of a Business Meeting 

**Who does what?**

1.  **Tasks of a Meeting**

> The first thing to understand about managing a meeting is that the people in the meeting have different tasks and responsibilities. Each task contributes to the success of the meeting.
>
> The five tasks are:

1.  Chairing

2.  Speaking

3.  Participating

4.  Taking notes

5.  Time keeping

> These tasks are described in detail on the following page.

2.  **The Key to Success**

> Everyone in a meeting is important. They need to come prepared, know the issues and be able to contribute with information, opinions, analysis, questions, comments and proposals.
>
> However, the success of a meeting depends on the chairperson:

- A good chairperson creates the opportunity for a successful meeting.

- A poor chairperson does not create such an opportunity and thus wastes everyone’s time.

- The successful meeting begins and ends with the chairperson.

<li><p>Create, review and follow the agenda.</p></li>
<li><p>Use appropriate language and courtesies.</p></li>
<li><p>Close the meeting with a summary and review of decisions.</p></li>
<li><p>Make it possible for all participants to speak and contribute to the discussion.</p></li>

</blockquote></td>

<p>The official <strong>note taker</strong> is an assistant to the chairperson.</p>
</blockquote>
<ul>

<li><p>The note taker might be a participant or observer of the meeting.</p></li>
</ul></td>

<td colspan="2" style="text-align: left;"><blockquote>
<p><strong>5. Time keeping</strong></p>
</blockquote></td>
<td style="text-align: left;"><blockquote>
<p>Good chairpeople are good <strong>timekeepers</strong>. Time management is essential in making a meeting successful.</p>
</blockquote>
<ul>
<li><p>Meetings need to start on time.</p></li>
<li><p>Enough time needs to be allocated for the meeting in order to deal effectively with the issues and achieve the goals.</p></li>
<li><p>Discussions and decisions cannot be rushed just because there is not enough time to deal with them properly.</p></li>
<li><p>Meetings need to end on time: they should not be too long as a result of poor time management.</p></li>
</ul></td>

</blockquote>
<ul>
<li><p>Observing is not a task in a meeting because observers do not participate in the meeting and do not influence the outcome.</p></li>
