# 7 Taking a Message

> *Getting the details*

- Business Situation: In the office, answering the phone

> Takashi Ikeda is working at his desk. The phone rings and he answers it.

1.  Taking a message

> Phone Ring-ring.
>
> Ikeda Good afternoon, Nippon Taiyo international sales. How may I help you?
>
> Caller Hello. This is Ted Klein from CM Fabrication. May I speak to Mari Inoue, please?
>
> Ikeda One moment, please, Mr. Klein. I’ll put you on hold. … Hello, Mr. Klein?
>
> Caller Yes?
>
> Ikeda Thank you for holding. I’m sorry, but Ms Inoue is in a meeting right now. May I take a message?
>
> Caller Yes, please. Could you ask Ms Inoue to call me as soon as possible, please? It’s about our meeting tomorrow.
>
> Ikeda May I have your number please, Mr. Klein?
>
> Caller Certainly. It’s 080-92-50-03-19.
>
> Ikeda Let me confirm. That’s 080-92-50-03-19?
>
> Caller Yes, that’s right.
>
> Ikeda And your name is Ted Klein and you are with CM Fabrication, is that right?
>
> Caller Yes, that’s right.
>
> Ikeda Thank you Mr. Klein. I’ll give Ms Inoue your message as soon as she returns from the meeting.
>
> Caller Thank you very much. Good-bye.
>
> Ikeda Thank you for calling. Good-bye.

- Expressions for the Telephone

1.  **Taking and leaving messages**

- May I take a message?

- Would you like to leave a message?

- Let me read that back to you.

- Could you read that back to me, please?

- A: Could you take a message for Ms Inoue, please?

> B: Yes, certainly. Go ahead, please.

- A: Could you ask Ms Inoue to call me back, please?

> B: Yes, certainly. I’ll give her your message.

2.  **Managing difficulties**

- I’m sorry. Could you repeat that, please?

> Could you spell that, please?
>
> Could you speak more slowly, please?
>
> Let me confirm.
>
> Yes, I understand.
>
> I’m sorry. Just a moment, please.

- Tips for Easier Phone Calls

Eye contact and gestures are important elements for effective face-to-face communication. On the telephone however, we have only the voice so it is very important to speak effectively. Here are some tips for speaking on the phone and making phone calls easier:

1.  Memorize essential fixed expressions for the telephone and use them.

2.  Keep a list of essential expressions near your phone.

3.  Listen carefully and take notes.

4.  Ask questions – if you ask questions you can get the information you need.

5.  Speak slowly and clearly, and emphasize key words.

6.  Speak smoothly – avoid hesitation.
