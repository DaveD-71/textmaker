# 9 Handling a Problem

> *Explaining changes & delays and apologizing*

- Business Situation: On the phone

> Takashi Ikeda needs to call his client, Amanda Jones, to change an appointment.

1.  Changing an appointment

> Phone Ring-ring.
>
> Jones Atlas Construction. May I help you?
>
> Ikeda Yes, could I speak with Amanda Jones, please?
>
> Jones Yes, speaking.
>
> Ikeda Oh! Good afternoon, Ms Jones. This is Takashi Ikeda at Nippon Taiyo.
>
> Jones Yes Mr. Ikeda. What can I do for you?
>
> Ikeda I’m calling about our meeting tomorrow at 3 o’clock.
>
> Jones Yes?
>
> Ikeda I’m very sorry, but I’m not going to be able to make it at that time. I’ve caught a very bad cold and I shouldn’t be meeting people.
>
> Jones I understand. I’m sorry to hear you’re not feeling well. When would you like to meet?
>
> Ikeda Could we meet next week on Tuesday or Wednesday?
>
> Jones Let me look at my calendar. How about Wednesday at 11?
>
> Ikeda Yes, Wednesday at 11 is good for me. Thank you Ms Jones. I’m very sorry for the inconvenience.
>
> Jones Not at all, Mr. Ikeda. I hope you’re feeling better soon.
>
> Ikeda Thank you Ms Jones. Good-bye.
>
> Jones Thank you for calling. Good-bye.

2.  Explaining and apologizing for a problem

> Phone Ring-ring.
>
> Staff Atlas Construction. May I help you?
>
> Ikeda Yes. This is Takashi Ikeda from Nippon Taiyo. May I speak to Amanda Jones, please?
>
> Staff Yes, just a moment please.
>
> Jones Amanda Jones speaking.
>
> Ikeda Hello, Ms Jones. This is Takashi Ikeda from Nippon Taiyo.
>
> Jones Yes, Mr. Ikeda, what can I do for you?
>
> Ikeda I’m calling about your order of wall panels.
>
> Jones Yes?
>
> Ikeda We’ve had some machine trouble at the plant and I’m very sorry but your shipment is going to be delayed.
>
> Jones I see. How much of a delay will there be?
>
> Ikeda Unfortunately, about three or four days.
>
> Jones How much of the order is finished?
>
> Ikeda We have half the order ready now. Would you like us to ship the first half of the order on schedule and the second half later?
>
> Jones Yes, would you do that please?
>
> Ikeda Yes, certainly. And we will take care of the extra shipping costs.
>
> Jones Thank you. Could you send me the revised shipping documents?
>
> Ikeda Yes, I’ll do that right away.
>
> Jones Thank you.
>
> Ikeda So, let me confirm. We’ll ship half your order on schedule on October 14th. And we’ll ship the second half as soon as it’s ready, probably the 18th. And we will take care of the extra shipping costs.
>
> Jones Yes, thank you. I understand.
>
> Ikeda Thank you very much, Ms Jones. I’m very sorry for the delay.
>
> Jones Thank you for calling and taking care of the problem.
>
> Ikeda Thank you very much Ms Jones. I appreciate your understanding. Good-bye.
>
> Jones Good-bye.
