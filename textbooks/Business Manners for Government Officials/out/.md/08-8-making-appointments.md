# 8 Making Appointments

Confirming the purpose / Setting the time & place

> <u>Situation: On the phone</u>

1.  Calling a counterparty

> Tomoya Ishii calls Alicia Phan at the European Safety Council for the first time to arrange a meeting.
>
> Phone Ring-ring.
>
> Phan Good morning. European Safety Council. Alicia Phan speaking. May I help you?
>
> Ishii Good morning, Ms Phan. My name is Tomoya Ishii. I’m from the Japan Industrial Safety Research Agency.
>
> Phan Yes, Mr. Ishii. What can I do for you?
>
> Ishii I’m going to be in Brussels in two weeks. If possible, I would like to come to your office to meet you.
>
> Phan Let me look at my schedule. One moment please… I can meet you two weeks from now on Thursday afternoon, the 26<sup>th</sup>. Is that convenient for you?
>
> Ishii Yes, Thursday the 26<sup>th</sup> would be fine. What time are you available?
>
> Phan I’m available from two-thirty.
>
> Ishii Could we meet at two-thirty, then?
>
> Phan Yes, two-thirty on the 26<sup>th</sup> is fine.
>
> Ishii Thank you Ms Phan. I’m looking forward to meeting you then. Good-bye.
>
> Phan I’m looking forward to meeting you too, Mr. Ishii. Thank you for calling. Good-bye.

2.  Calling a manufacturer

> Ayako Mori is working in the Tokyo office of the ISRA. She receives a call from a footwear manufacturer to arrange a meeting.
>
> Phone Ring-ring.
>
> Mori Good morning, Japan Industrial Safety Research Agency. May I help you?
>
> Iman Good morning. My name is Tamin Iman from Sprint Footwear in Bangladesh.
>
> Mori Yes, Mr. Iman. What can I do for you?
>
> Iman I’m in Tokyo for a few days and I would like to meet with you to discuss your industrial safety training programs.
>
> Mori I see.
>
> Iman Could we meet tomorrow?
>
> Mori Tomorrow? No, I’m very sorry but I’ll be away on business tomorrow. Could we meet on Thursday?
>
> Iman I’m sorry no. I’m leaving Tokyo on Thursday morning. Is Wednesday morning possible?
>
> Mori Let me look at my calendar. I’m sorry, the morning is not possible but I could meet you in the afternoon on Wednesday at 1 o’clock? Is that convenient for you?
>
> Iman Yes, 1 o’clock on Wednesday is convenient. Thank you very much Ms Mori.
>
> Mori Not at all. I’ll see you then.
>
> Iman Thank you Ms Mori. Good-bye.
>
> Mori Thank you for calling Mr. Iman. Good-bye.
>

- **Available Vs. Convenient**

> Available and convenient refer to the same thing: It is possible to do something at a particular time or place.
>

> <u>Telephone Etiquette</u>

- **Saying good-bye**

> In the situation in Dialog 1, notice that Mr. Ishii says good-bye first.
