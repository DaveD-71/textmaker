# 5 Having a Meeting

Basic procedure for business meetings

> <u>Basic procedure for business meetings</u>
>
> Effective business meetings go through six stages. Each stage contributes to the efficient use of time and the success of the meeting.

- Meetings go through six stages:

1.  Begin with courtesies and appreciations.

2.  Confirm the purpose and goal of the meeting.

3.  Confirm the items for discussion. The list of items for discussion in a meeting is called “the agenda”.

4.  Conduct the meeting according to the agenda.

5.  Review the results of the discussion and confirm any decisions.

6.  Close the meeting with courtesies and appreciations.

> <u>Model Business Meeting</u>
>
> The model meeting transcript below follows the six stages of a meeting and uses common expressions for each stage.
>
> Situation: Tomoya Ishii has a meeting with Alicia Phan at her office. He requested the meeting to give her ISRA’s latest research report and to ask about a conference on industrial safety. The discussion begins in the meeting room.
