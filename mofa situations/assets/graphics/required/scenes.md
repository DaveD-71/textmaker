# Scene Asset Brief Sheets

*(Top 10 reusable Roleplay-1 scenes)*

---

## SCENE S26

### Travel Information: Directions & Transport Options

**Applies to situations**
H09, G02, G04, F03, F05, F07, E05, E10

**Core interaction**
Staff explains how to get somewhere, including routes or transport choices.

**People (2)**

* Japanese embassy / consulate staff
* Local person (visitor or caller)

**Primary location (decisioned)**

* Embassy / consulate office OR reception
* Use **office desk** when phone-based; **reception counter** when visitor is present

**Furniture & props (minimal)**

* Desk or reception counter
* One paper map / route sheet (generic, no text)

**Body language**

* Staff pointing lightly at paper or gesturing direction
* Visitor listening attentively

**Do not include**

* Vehicles
* City landmarks
* Arrows drawn into the scene

**Negative space**

* Upper-right or right margin (≈30%)
