# Batch 1 — article shape & opening plan

Assigned before drafting, for designed variety (house-style.md §6). Prototypes 1.1 already: A = dated-event open / problem→responses; B = plain-definition open / data-led.

## Book A — Let's Talk Finance

| Topic | Article shape | Opening style | Lead region(s) | Japan placement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.2 CBDCs | data-led explainer | surprising number ("9 in 10 central banks...") | global (BIS survey), then China / euro area / Bahamas-Jamaica-Nigeria | one sentence: BOJ pilot since 2023, no decision |
| 1.3 Fintech Regulation | two-model comparison (broad statutory vs patchwork) | short comparison (UK sandbox 2016 vs US patchwork) | UK, EU, India, US | mid-article: 2017 open-API law + tiered funds-transfer licences |
| 1.4 Data Privacy | problem → responses (the data-use vs protection tension) | concrete case (Meta EUR 1.2bn transfer fine) | EU, US (Equifax/Capital One), then Japan/India | short para: APPI, PPC, EU-Japan mutual adequacy 2019 |
| 1.5 Financial Inclusion | two-world contrast (building access vs changing how you pay) | a number that reframes ("~1.3bn adults still have no account") | Sub-Saharan Africa (M-Pesa/Kenya), India (PMJDY), Brazil (Pix) | closing third: Japan = different meaning, cash use + Japan Post Bank |

## Book B — Let's Talk Finance 2

| Topic | Article shape | Opening style | Lead region(s) | Japan placement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.2 Consumer Debt | data-led explainer w/ a mechanism (compounding) | concrete contrast (card ~21% vs mortgage ~6.7%) | US (scale), UK (BNPL rule), South Korea (high leverage) | mid: 2006 Money Lending Act cap, lump-sum vs revolving norm |
| 1.3 Saving & Investing | mechanism-led (compounding + the index-vs-active fact) | a common misconception ("saving and investing are the same") | global (125-yr real returns), US (SPIVA), UK/US/AU accounts | closing third: Japan ~50% cash/deposits, new NISA 2024 take-up |
| 1.4 Housing | two-case comparison (prices rise vs prices fall) | a direct question ("Does a house always gain value?") | NZ/Canada (doubled then fell), Germany (Berlin rent cap), then Japan | second half: Japan = depreciating buildings, akiya, 800k starts |
| 1.5 Scams & Fraud | problem → responses (losses vs countermeasures) | a scale number ("US$12.5bn reported lost in the US in 2024") | US, UK (reimbursement rule), Australia (losses fell), SE Asia (compounds) | mid: Japan special fraud ¥72bn record, elderly targeting, ATM limits |
