# Batch 2 — article shape & opening plan

Part 2 of both books. Assigned before drafting, for designed variety (house-style.md §6). Checked against Batch 1's openings so no style is used by more than ~3 topics per book.

## Book A — Let's Talk Finance, Part 2 (Markets, Institutions and Financial Stability)

| Topic | Article shape | Opening style | Lead region(s) | Japan placement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 Regulatory Responses to Market Volatility | short chronology / timeline | a specific market event (e.g. a flash crash or a 2020 circuit-breaker day) | US (circuit breakers), then EU, Japan (TSE), other Asia | mid: TSE circuit-breaker / special-quote rules; 1990s crash context |
| 2.2 Credit Rating Agencies Oversight | problem → responses (conflict of interest → post-2008 rules) | a common misconception ("a rating is a fact / a guarantee") | US (SEC, NRSRO), EU (ESMA registration), then Japan (JCR/R&I), one EM sovereign downgrade | short para: Japan's own agencies (JCR, R&I) + FSA registration |
| 2.3 Insurance Market Regulation | data-led explainer (what solvency rules do) | a concrete failure or near-failure (e.g. an insurer that could not pay claims) | EU (Solvency II), US (state-based, NAIC), Japan (FSA solvency margin), UK | mid: Japan life-insurer failures around 2000; solvency-margin standard |
| 2.4 Stress Testing and Risk Management | two-case comparison (a test that worked vs a risk it missed) | a direct question ("Can a bank survive a bad year on paper?") | US (CCAR/DFAST), EU (EBA), Bank of England, then Japan (BOJ/FSA) | short para: BOJ/FSA joint stress scenarios |
| 2.5 Sovereign Debt Management | data-led explainer (how a government borrows) | a number that reframes (a big debt-to-GDP figure, e.g. Japan's) | Japan (very high debt, mostly domestic), US, one EM in distress, euro area | LEAD case: Japan's high but domestically-held debt — genuine standout, use it as the anchor not an aside |

## Book B — Let's Talk Finance 2, Part 2 (Companies, Work and Money)

| Topic | Article shape | Opening style | Lead region(s) | Japan placement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 How Companies Raise Money | data-led explainer (equity vs debt, public vs private) | a concrete contrast (a company that listed vs one that stayed private) | US (IPO market), Japan (TSE listings), Europe, one EM exchange | mid: TSE Prime/Standard/Growth; Japanese firms' bank-debt tradition |
| 2.2 Startups, Venture Capital and Failure | short chronology (the funding ladder, seed → Series A → exit or failure) | a scale/attrition number ("most startups fail — here's the rate") | US (Silicon Valley), then Europe, India/SE Asia, Japan | short para: Japan's smaller but growing VC scene; unicorn count |
| 2.3 The Gig Economy and Income Security | problem → responses (irregular income → classification fights) | a scene / concrete situation (a delivery rider's week) | UK (worker-status rulings), EU (platform-work directive), US (California AB5/Prop 22), Japan | mid: Japan's freelance-protection law (2024); gig platforms in Japan |
| 2.4 Executive Pay and Inequality Inside Firms | two-case comparison (a pay revolt vs a pay package that passed) | a specific number (a CEO-to-median pay ratio) | US (say-on-pay, pay-ratio disclosure), UK, Japan (lower ratios, rising), Europe | short para: Japan's historically low exec pay, disclosure rules, recent rises |
| 2.5 Banks: What They Do and How They Fail | data-led explainer with a mechanism (maturity transformation → a run) | a dated event (a 2023 bank failure, e.g. SVB, told briefly) | US (2023 regional failures), Europe (Credit Suisse), then Japan, deposit-insurance systems | mid: Japan's deposit insurance (DICJ), 1990s–2000s bank crisis briefly |
