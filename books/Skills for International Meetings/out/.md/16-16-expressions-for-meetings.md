# 16 Expressions for Meetings

> *Study, practice, & use*

1.  **<u>Chairing</u>**

<!-- -->

1.  **Getting everyone’s attention if necessary**

> May I have your attention, please? … It is time to begin … Thank you.

2.  **Reviewing the purpose and agenda**

<!-- -->

1.  I’d like to review the agenda.

2.  We have three speakers this afternoon.

<!-- -->

3.  **Introducing the speaker**

<!-- -->

1.  It is a great pleasure to introduce our (first / next / final) speaker this afternoon.

2.  His name is Mr. Takeshi Ono. Mr. Ono is … (background)

3.  Mr. Ono, if you please. (Calling on the speaker to begin.)

<!-- -->

4.  **Conducting the discussion**

<!-- -->

1.  Thank you, Mr. Ono. We have time for some questions.

2.  Are there any questions for Mr. Ono?

3.  Are there any more questions for Mr. Ono?

4.  Mr. Suzuki, do you have any questions for Mr. Ono?

<!-- -->

5.  **Taking a break**

<!-- -->

1.  It’s time for a break. We’ll begin again in 10 minutes.

2.  We’ll we take a short break now. We’ll start again in 10 minutes.

<!-- -->

6.  **Starting after a break**

<!-- -->

1.  It’s time to start again.

2.  We’ll continue with …

<!-- -->

7.  **Wrapping up the discussion**

<!-- -->

1.  We have time for one more question.

2.  I’m sorry, that’s all the time we have for questions.

3.  I’m sorry, we’re all out of time.

4.  I’m sorry, we have to bring this to a close.

5.  I’m sorry, this is very interesting but we have to move on to our next item / the next item on our agenda.

6.  Thank you everyone.

7.  Thank you for a very interesting presentation, Mr. Ono.

8.  That was a very interesting report, Mr. Ono. Thank you very much.

<!-- -->

2.  **<u>Speaker’s response to the chairperson</u>**

<!-- -->

1.  Thank you, Mr. Gerrard.

2.  It’s my pleasure.

3.  My pleasure.

4.  … Good afternoon, everyone.

<!-- -->

3.  **<u>Vocabulary for Questions and Discussion</u>**

<!-- -->

1.  **Introducing your question**

<!-- -->

1.  I have a question.

<!-- -->

4.  I’d like to ask (you) about…

<!-- -->

5.  I have two questions. First … Thank you. And my second question is…

<!-- -->

2.  **Interrupting a discussion to ask a question**

<!-- -->

1.  I’m sorry. May I ask a question?

<!-- -->

2.  I’m sorry. Could I ask a question?

<!-- -->

3.  I’m sorry. I’d like to ask a question.

<!-- -->

3.  **Questions for clarification**

<!-- -->

1.  I think you said … Is that correct?

<!-- -->

2.  I think you told us that … Am I right?

<!-- -->

3.  Did you say …?

<!-- -->

4.  I'm sorry. You said something about … but I didn't understand it.

<!-- -->

5.  Did you say something about …?

<!-- -->

4.  **More information**

<!-- -->

1.  Could you tell me more about …?

<!-- -->

2.  I’d like to get more information about …

<!-- -->

3.  I'm sorry. You told us about … Could you give us some more details?

<!-- -->

5.  **Questions to challenge and test**

<!-- -->

1.  I'm sorry, you said something about …

<!-- -->

2.  … Could you tell us what you mean?

<!-- -->

3.  … Could you be more specific?

<!-- -->

4.  What’s the connection between <u>A</u> and <u>B</u>?

<!-- -->

5.  I’m sorry; I don’t see the connection between <u>A</u> and <u>B</u>.

<!-- -->

6.  What do you think about …?

<!-- -->

7.  <u>What</u> do you <u>think</u> about this?

<!-- -->

9.  <u>How</u> do you <u>feel</u> about …? (Note: “<u>How</u> do you think about …?” is not correct.)

<!-- -->

10. I’d like to get your opinion on …

<!-- -->

11. I think ... What's your opinion?

<!-- -->

11. You said … but don’t you think that ...?

<!-- -->

12. Don't you agree?

13. Don't you think so?

14. Don't you think that…?

15. Wouldn't you say that …?

16. Wouldn't you agree that …?

<!-- -->

4.  **<u>Responding to Questions</u>**

<!-- -->

1.  **Pausing to think**

If you need time to think about a response, you must say something. Silence is rude.

1.  Thank you for asking that.

2.  That's an interesting question.

3.  Let me see.

4.  Let me think about that.

<!-- -->

2.  **Clarifying a question**

Don’t try to answer a question if you don’t understand it. Always ask for clarification.

1.  I’m sorry, could you repeat your question, please?

2.  Could you say that again, please?

3.  I'm sorry, could you rephrase your question, please?

4.  I'm sorry. I don't understand your question.

5.  Did you say …?

<!-- -->

3.  **Responding to questions**

Avoid answering questions with just one word, “Yes” or “No”. Short blunt answers do not create an interactive atmosphere. Always expand on a simple answer.

1.  Yes, that's right.

2.  No. I think you misunderstand. Let me clarify / explain.

3.  I'd like to explain.

4.  I'm sorry. I don't have any more information about that.

<!-- -->

4.  **Giving opinions**

<!-- -->

1.  I think that …

2.  I feel that …

3.  Personally, I feel / believe / think that …

4.  It seems to me that …

<!-- -->

5.  **Agreeing with an opinion or suggestion**

When you agree, you should summarize the points of agreement and explain why.

1.  I agree with you because …

2.  I agree with what you are saying because …

3.  I agree that we should …

4.  I see what you mean.

5.  You've got a point.

6.  You're right about that.

<!-- -->

6.  **Disagreeing with an opinion or suggestion**

<!-- -->

1.  I see what you mean, but I (still) can't agree with you.

2.  I agree that something should be done, however, …

3.  I agree with your point that … but …

4.  That's true, but …

5.  I'm afraid I'm not entirely convinced.

6.  No, I don't think that's right.

7.  I'm sorry I don't agree.

8.  I'm afraid I can't agree with you.

<!-- -->

7.  **For responding to answers**

<!-- -->

1.  I understand, thank you.

2.  I see, thank you.

3.  I’m sorry. I’m not sure what you mean.

<!-- -->

5.  **<u>Reaching a decision</u>**

<!-- -->

1.  **Expressing agreement with a decision**

<!-- -->

1.  I agree with the decision.

2.  We are all agreed that we need to…

3.  So, everyone agrees with this idea. Right?

4.  Okay, now that we have agreement on this issue, let’s talk about…

<!-- -->

2.  **Expressing disagreement with a decision**

<!-- -->

1.  I’m sorry, but I don’t agree with this decision. My main reason is....

2.  Actually, I don’t believe this is the right decision. If we do this, then….

<!-- -->

3.  **When external factors prevent a response**

<!-- -->

1.  The suggestion is worth putting forward to the committee. Let’s write up the proposal and submit it before the deadline. (*Beyond the authority of the group.)*

2.  We need to check the viability of the proposal with the Budget Committee first. Let’s table this decision until we have approval from them. *(When input is required from members outside the meeting.)  *
