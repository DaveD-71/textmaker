- **Purpose of Skills for International Meetings & Conferences**

<!-- -->

- The purpose of *Skills for International Meetings & Conferences* is to learn the structure, tasks and language of meetings.

- The training procedure is to:

1.  Study and understand the structure and task of business meetings.

2.  Practice the structure, tasks, and language with model meeting transcripts.

3.  Practice the skills in simulations.

- **Simulations**

<!-- -->

- The purpose of simulations is to practice the structure, tasks and language of meetings. Time window: each simulation has a time window that includes the meeting simulation and follow up feedback.

- The target skills for the training programme are practiced and rotated amongst students in order to ensure everyone has an equal opportunity to learn the skills and knowledge and apply them.

<!-- -->

- **Preparation of Simulations**

<!-- -->

- Topics are decided in advance, and students are given an opportunity to prepare for the simulation by gathering information to be presented.

- Students should review the model transcripts prior to the simulation in order to refamiliarise themselves with the meetings tasks and procedures, and related vocabulary.

- The objective of the simulation is to practice and apply recently learned knowledge, so students should go into the simulation with a clear understanding of what they want to achieve: If the goal is to use and activate new phrases or vocabulary, then those phrases should be prepared in advance. If the goal is to practice a new meeting technique, e.g. reaching a decision, or interrupting a conversation, then preparation for these skills is also necessary. If the goal is to practice a task, such as chairing a meeting, or presenting a topic, then these too need to be prepared. The type and amount of preparation required will vary depending on the goal. The instructor will advise the students of what is necessary.
