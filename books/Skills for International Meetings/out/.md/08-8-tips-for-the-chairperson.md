# 8 Tips for the Chairperson

> *How to be an effective chairperson*

<img src="assets/media/image4.png" style="width:2.45157in;height:1.53858in" />

- **Chairing Tips: Before the Meeting**

<table style="width:88%;">
<colgroup>
<col style="width: 88%" />
</colgroup>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><ol type="1">
<li><p><strong>Be prepared!</strong></p></li>
</ol>
<p>Know what you have to do and know what you are going to say.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Know the purpose, agenda, and timeframe for the meeting.</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><ol type="1">
<li><p><strong>Documents</strong></p></li>
</ol>
<p>Make sure you have all the necessary documents such as the agenda, speakers’ list and introduction notes, and schedules.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Check all the documents carefully. Make sure you have all the information you need, that it is correct, and that you understand it.</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><ol type="1">
<li><p><strong>Speakers</strong></p></li>
</ol>
<p>Meet the speakers and key participants briefly before your meeting.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Find out how the speakers would like to be introduced. The chairperson’s introduction is an important courtesy and part of giving the speakers credibility.</p></li>
<li><p>Confirm the details of their names, organizations, titles, and the titles of their talks.</p></li>
<li><p>Learn how to pronounce their names correctly.</p></li>
<li><p>Review the agenda and time frame with the key participants.</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><ol type="1">
<li><p><strong>Technicians</strong></p></li>
</ol>
<p>Meet the technicians and others who are assisting you.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Review the procedure for the meeting.</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><ol type="1">
<li><p><strong>Equipment</strong></p></li>
</ol>
<p>Check the audio-visual equipment.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Make sure the microphones and other audio-visual equipment are working properly before the meeting begins.</p></li>
<li><p>Even though others are responsible for the equipment, if something goes wrong during your meeting you will have to take care of the situation.</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

- **Chairing Tips: During the Meeting**

<table style="width:88%;">
<colgroup>
<col style="width: 88%" />
</colgroup>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><ol type="1">
<li><p><strong>Body language</strong></p></li>
</ol>
<p>Manage the meeting through eye contact and body language.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>For a large meeting, it is easier and also the standard protocol to stand while chairing.</p></li>
<li><p>For smaller meetings and roundtable discussions the chair is usually seated.</p></li>
<li><p>Pay attention to the speakers and the audience at all times. Never look down, look away, or appear distracted.</p></li>
<li><p>Inattention is rude to the speakers and will distract the audience from the speaker.</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><ol type="1">
<li><p><strong>Questions</strong></p></li>
</ol>
<p>Be prepared to ask questions.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>If there are no questions from the audience at the beginning of the Q&amp;A session the chair must be prepared to ask questions.</p></li>
<li><p>Your questions will stimulate other questions.</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><ol type="1">
<li><p><strong>Take Notes</strong></p></li>
</ol>
<p>Take notes during the meeting.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Give a brief summary of the key points and results (decisions) at the end of the meeting.</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><ol type="1">
<li><p><strong>Conclusion</strong></p></li>
</ol>
<p>Bring discussions to a clear conclusion before moving on.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Restate the conclusions clearly and simply so everyone understands them.</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
