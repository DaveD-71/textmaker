- **International Conferences**

> Large international conferences are typically composed of four different types of meetings. Each type of meeting plays an important role in achieving the goals of the conference.

1.  **Plenary Sessions**

> Large conferences open and close with formal meetings called *plenary sessions*. All the participants attend the plenary sessions.
>
> The opening plenary session introduces the themes and purpose of the conference
>
> The closing plenary session wraps up the conference by reviewing the accomplishments with a view to the future.
>
> Please go to Unit 3 for more details and a model transcript.

2.  **Panel Discussions**

> A *panel discussion* is a discussion among experts with differing ideas on one issue. The discussion is coordinated by a moderator (chairperson).

- Panels are usually made up of 4 or 5 speakers. Panels with more than five speakers become difficult to manage.

- Typically, speakers sit side by side, sometimes at a long table. Each speaker should have a microphone.

- The moderator might sit in the middle of the speakers; sit to one side of the speakers; or for large meetings might stand to one side in order to see the audience more easily.

- Each speaker presents his/her position in turn and then engages in discussion with the other members of the panel: agreeing, disagreeing, and adding to the others’ comments.

- The moderator has a set of questions to ask the panel members to stimulate the discussion.

- Members of the audience can also ask questions of the panel or individual members.

- The recommended time allotment for a panel discussion is 45 minutes.

3.  **Small Group Meetings**

> Plenary sessions are important for opening and closing large conferences but the real work of the conference is done in *small group meetings*. There are two types of small group meetings:

- *Roundtable discussions* for exchanging information

- *Working Groups* for discussing specific issues, considering proposals and making decisions.

- Please go to Units 4 and 5 for more details on how these meetings are conducted and model transcripts.

4.  **Meeting People 1-on-1**

> Besides the scheduled meetings, meeting other participants 1-on-1 in receptions, parties, during meals, and in breaks between the sessions are a very important part of international conferences.

- Please go to Units 6 & 7 for the language and protocol for various types of 1-on-1 meetings and model transcripts.
