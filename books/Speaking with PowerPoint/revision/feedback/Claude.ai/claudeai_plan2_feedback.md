Plan 2 is a real improvement — it directly fixes the structural problem I flagged and closes out the P0 decisions instead of leaving them open. A few things worth double-checking before you sign off.

**What got fixed well:**

- **Scope mismatch resolved.** It states plainly that this is a Presentation Skills series rebuild, not a book edit, and explicitly retires the two-day sprint as "a fallback editorial triage lens, not the production schedule." No more silent pivot.
- **All four P0 decisions are now closed.** Target learner (B1–B2 adult business ESL, Japan), model-case strategy (three recurring strands instead of one Ventura replacement), scope (series, not book), and title (explicitly deferred, not just implied) are all stated as decisions rather than left for an agent to infer mid-run.
- **Licensing and accessibility are now per-asset requirements**, not just aspirational content — every asset needs source/licensing status, alt text, and a contrast/color-blind check. That addresses the gap where you could teach accessibility while shipping inaccessible diagrams.
- **Essentials/Long are now actually defined** relative to Standard (compression rule for Essentials, named extensions for Long) — previously that relationship was implicit.
- **Answer key/trainer notes and a decision log are new deliverables** — good additions that weren't in Plan 1's deliverable list.

**One real content gap: AI guidance seems to have dropped out.**

Both original audits flagged "add AI-assisted presentation guidance" as a P1 (must-fix) item — using AI to draft/design/critique slides, with a responsibility/fact-checking checklist. Plan 1 had "AI-first example: Gamma, with caution about company policy and confidentiality" in its tool list. Plan 2's core tool list (PowerPoint, Google Slides, Canva, Keynote, generic formats) has no AI-drafting tool example, and scanning the 12-lesson map, none of the lessons mention AI tools, AI-generated slide critique, or an "AI responsibility" checklist. Unless this is deliberately folded into Lesson 7 (Tool and Format Choice) without being named, it looks like a regression from a P1 item to nothing. Worth confirming this was an intentional cut and not a drop.

**Smaller things to check:**

- **No timeline or effort budget at all now.** Retiring the two-day sprint was the right call given the scope shift, but the plan doesn't replace it with any schedule or phase-level effort estimate — worth adding even a rough one so "production-ready" also means "schedulable."
- **No rollback/versioning practice** for the existing series content before agents start rewriting unit JSON — still absent from Plan 1's version, still absent here.
- A few original P1 items (pointer/cursor guidance, backup/export/security advice) aren't visible anywhere in the 12-lesson map or QA checklist — they probably fit under Lesson 8 or 9, but it's not explicit, so there's a risk they quietly fall out the way AI guidance seems to have.
- The external review step is now specified as "one structured review round" (better than before), but there's still no stated criterion for what happens if that round finds a significant issue — another round, or ship with corrections noted?
