   - Assigned support-desk staff

   - Repeat inquiries

Slide 2

Last month:

- 312 inquiries about the resident certificate support form
- 47% about attachments or field entry
- 28% led to repeat contact

Service issue:

- Residents need help before they submit the form.

---

Slide 3

Pilot service flow:

1. User starts online form.
2. User asks the support desk for help.
3. User submits form.
4. Staff review form.

What does not change:

- Staff do not complete the form for the resident.
- Staff do not make the application decision.
- Application requirements stay the same.

---

Slide 4

The support desk should create three benefits:

1. Fewer preventable errors
2. Fewer repeat contacts
3. Clearer guidance from staff

Learning benefit:

- Common questions are recorded each week.

---

Slide 5

Three-month pilot:

1. Month 1
   - Open Tuesday and Thursday mornings.
2. Month 2
   - Review common questions and update guidance.
3. Month 3
   - Review workload and user comments.

Target:

- 15% fewer repeat inquiries
