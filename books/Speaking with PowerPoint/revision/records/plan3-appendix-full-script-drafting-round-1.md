## Main Drafting Constraints

- Scripts must be complete spoken model presentations, not only scenario packs or outlines.
- Each model family must include one business-client finance/trading operations variant and one government-agency administrative variant.
- AI must not be promoted as a shortcut or replacement for English-language development.
- Finance/trading examples must avoid investment advice, market predictions, ticker symbols, real firm names, real client/account data, and regulatory/legal advice.
- Government examples must stay in administrative work and avoid political advocacy, legislation, budget campaigning, flags, seals, emblems, and crests.
- Teaching points must logically fit the model topic.
