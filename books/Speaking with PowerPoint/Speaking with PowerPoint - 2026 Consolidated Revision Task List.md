## Executive Decision

The two AI audits agree on the largest problem: the book reads like a 2014-2016 formal speech manual with PowerPoint attached. The 2026 rewrite should reframe it as a practical business-presentation course: help listeners understand information, make decisions, approve proposals, act on recommendations, or discuss next steps.

The revised course should not be organized around PowerPoint as the default or prescribed tool. PowerPoint can remain one example because many companies still use it, but the larger teaching target should be "presenting with visuals" across modern tools and delivery environments: PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote, Canva, Figma Slides, Pitch, Gamma, PDF decks, dashboards, screen-shared documents, video-conference presentation modes, and AI-assisted slide generators. The durable skills are message, structure, visual thinking, evidence, delivery, interaction, and tool choice.

The main disagreement is scope. Claude.ai recommends preserving the current pedagogy and doing a fast modernization. ChatGPT recommends a deeper rebuild. The practical compromise is:

1. Day 1 must fix the conceptual frame, delivery context, slide-design advice, and dated Ventura model.
2. Day 2 must replace visible dated production elements, add accessibility/AI/data/online-delivery coverage, and run a consistency pass.
3. Anything that requires a full new course architecture should be captured as a future-edition task unless it blocks the 2026 credibility of the current edition.

## Priority Scale

- P0: Decide before rewriting. The rest of the work depends on it.
- P1: Must fix in the two-day sprint. Leaving it unchanged makes the book feel outdated or misleading.
- P2: Should fix in the two-day sprint if possible. High value, but not always blocking.
- P3: Polish or future-edition work.

## Two-Day Sprint Plan

### Day 1 - Content and Structure

1. Make the P0 decisions: Ventura strategy, update vs rebuild, target learner.
2. Rewrite the opening frame: business presentation, audience outcome, purpose, message, decision/action.
3. Revise Unit 2: flexible presentation structures instead of "all speeches" and three fixed points.
4. Replace or update the Logic Tree as a planning map.
5. Modernize or replace the Ventura model text and data.
6. Rewrite Unit 10 visual-design guidance: tool choice, format choice, message-led visuals, modern templates, fonts, visual hierarchy, Rule of 1, no rigid Rule of 7.
7. Draft new sections/checklists for AI, accessibility, data storytelling, and online/hybrid delivery.

### Day 2 - Production, Activities, and Quality

1. Rebuild model visuals in a modern, tool-agnostic style.
2. Add guided practice tasks and a final rubric.
3. Update delivery and Q&A sections for in-person, online, and hybrid contexts.
4. Refresh icons, repeated presenter imagery, Sample A/B, and page layout.
5. Update front/back matter, PDF metadata, revision line, and credits.
6. Fix cross-references, terminology, typos, capitalization, punctuation, spacing, and page density.
7. Run a final check against the task list and ensure the book no longer reads as a 2016 speech manual.

## Material Worth Preserving

Do not discard these strengths while modernizing:

- Clear purpose and message.
- Basic opening, development, and close.
- Signposting/Flag Expressions for ESL learners.
- Preparation and rehearsal emphasis.
- Advice not to read slides aloud.
- Explaining visuals instead of merely showing them.
- Model followed by learner application.
- Clarifying and responding to questions.
- Japan-specific pronunciation/register support, if made accurate and current.

## Minimum Viable 2026 Pass

If time becomes extremely tight, complete these ten tasks first:

1. Reframe the book from "speeches" to business presentations.
2. Make audience outcome/decision/action central.
3. Modernize or replace the Ventura case and remove 2014/2013 framing.
4. Rewrite the visual-design/tool-choice unit for 2026.
5. Replace obsolete font, template, and PowerPoint-only guidance.
6. Add online/hybrid delivery.
7. Add accessibility.
8. Add AI-responsibility guidance.

10. Correct front/back matter, metadata, cross-references, and known typos.
