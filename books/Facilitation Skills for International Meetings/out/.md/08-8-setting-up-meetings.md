# 8 Setting up Meetings

- **Plan for success**

> A successful meeting begins with the preparations.
>
> Giving the participants clear and complete information about the purpose and goal of the meeting is part of the foundation for success.
>
> Use the following two model e-mails as templates for your own e-mail.
>
> **Model E-mail 1**

Below is an e-mail to set up a staff meeting about cross cultural meeting skills.

You will find the meeting transcript (#1) in Unit 9.
