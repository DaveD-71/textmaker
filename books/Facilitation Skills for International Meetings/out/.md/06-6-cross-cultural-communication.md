# 6 Cross Cultural Communication

- **The Challenge of Cross-Cultural Communication**

<!-- -->

- Cultural differences in the protocol of handling conflict and making decisions are barriers to successful meetings.

- The facilitator cannot ignore the differences or assume that because the meeting is taking place in a certain location everyone will automatically follow the local protocol.

- Awareness of cultural diversity and how to bring people together are essential for effective facilitators.

- **Protocol of Communication**

<!-- -->

- Effective communication is much more than just vocabulary and grammar.

> Effective communication in any culture, in any language, depends on following the accepted rules of communication for that culture.
>
> These rules are called the protocol of communication.
>
> Protocol consists of -
>
> **Content What** to say and do.
>
> **Timing When** to say it and do it.
>
> **Style How** to say it and do it.

- Protocol is a procedure that helps create good relationships. It is an expression of respect and a willingness to communicate.

- **Three steps to overcoming conflict in cross-cultural meetings**

> If a meeting is well planned and well managed, conflict will be easier to handle.
>
> Create a successful cross-cultural meeting in three steps.

1.  Acknowledge that cultural differences exist among the participants and ask everyone to cooperate in order to create a successful meeting.

- Ask for the participants’ agreement.

2.  Clearly explain the meeting’s protocol for discussion and decision making. (The 6 Stages on page 8)

- Ask the participants to confirm their understanding.

3.  Conduct the meeting strictly according to the protocol.

- Treat everyone equally and fairly.

- Equal and fair treatment will re-enforce the protocol.
