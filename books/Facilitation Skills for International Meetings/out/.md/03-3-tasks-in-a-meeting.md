# 3 Tasks in a Meeting

> <img src="assets/media/image5.png" style="width:4.09028in;height:1.53819in" />
>
> **The people attending a meeting have different tasks to perform.**
>
> Meetings succeed because each person knows and completes his or her task.

- **Five tasks in a meeting**

<table style="width:90%;">
<colgroup>
<col style="width: 23%" />
<col style="width: 66%" />
</colgroup>
<thead>
<tr>
<th><blockquote>
<p><strong>1. Facilitator</strong></p>
</blockquote></th>
<th style="text-align: left;"><p>Before the meeting:</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Confirms that the participants are informed of the meeting’s date and time as well as the purpose, issues, and agenda.</p></li>
</ul>
<p>During the meeting:</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Manages the meeting from beginning to end.</p></li>
<li><p>Encourages participants to ask questions and offer comments.</p></li>
<li><p>Directs the flow of the discussion and guides it toward a conclusion.</p></li>
<li><p>Makes decisions about the meeting process but does not make decisions about the issues being discussed.</p></li>
</ul></th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><blockquote>
<p><strong>2. Topic Leader</strong></p>
</blockquote></td>
<td>Provides information necessary for the discussion.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><blockquote>
<p><strong>3. Participants</strong></p>
</blockquote></td>
<td><p>Prepare for the meeting.</p>
<p>Listen to the topic leader.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Evaluate the information.</p></li>
<li><p>Ask questions.</p></li>
<li><p>Make comments.</p></li>
<li><p>Contribute to the conclusion and decision making.</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><blockquote>
<p><strong>4. Note taker</strong></p>
</blockquote></td>
<td><p>Usually an assistant to the facilitator.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Often helps to set up the meeting.</p></li>
<li><p>Records the decisions during the meeting.</p></li>
<li><p>Reports the decisions during the meeting.</p></li>
<li><p>Prepares a report and distributes it to the participants.</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><blockquote>
<p><strong>5. Timekeeper</strong></p>
</blockquote></td>
<td><p>Meetings must start and finish on time.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>The timekeeper assists the facilitator by keeping track of the time and advising the facilitator.</p></li>
<li><p>The facilitator can act as the timekeeper.</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

- **Types of Meetings**

> Companies hold different types of meetings for different purposes: some meetings are for exchanging information, while other meetings are for making plans and decisions.

- It is essential for the facilitator and the participants to know what type of meeting they are attending.
