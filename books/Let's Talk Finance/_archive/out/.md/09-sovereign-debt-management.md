## 9. Sovereign Debt Management

### Reading

Sovereign debt management refers to the process by which governments borrow funds from domestic and international financial markets to finance budget deficits, infrastructure projects, and public services while maintaining fiscal sustainability and debt affordability.

Governments issue sovereign bonds, treasury bills, and other debt instruments to investors, who lend money in exchange for fixed interest payments and eventual repayment of principal. Sovereign debt management involves strategic decisions on debt issuance, debt maturity structure, and currency composition to minimize borrowing costs, manage risks, and ensure liquidity. Moreover, governments implement debt sustainability analyses, fiscal rules, and debt management strategies to monitor and control public debt levels relative to GDP, government revenues, and external financing sources.

Effective sovereign debt management practices require coordination between fiscal policy objectives, monetary policy stance, and debt management operations to support macroeconomic stability, investor confidence, and sustainable economic growth.
