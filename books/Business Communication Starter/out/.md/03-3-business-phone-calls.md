# 3. Business Phone Calls

## 1. Answering the Phone

Use the conversation models below as a guide and **practice answering the phone**.

#### Answering the Phone

| 🕿      | Ring-ring.                                                      |
|--------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Ikeda  | Good morning, Nippon Taiyo international sales. May I help you? |
| Caller | Yes. May I speak to Mr. Ikeda, please?                          |
| Ikeda  | Yes, speaking. How may I help you?                              |
| Caller | Hello, Mr. Ikeda. This is Amanda Jones from Atlas Construction. |
| Ikeda  | Oh hello, Ms Jones. What can I do for you?                      |
| Caller | I’m calling about …                                             |

#### Transferring the Call

| 🕿 | Ring-ring. |
|----|----|
| Ikeda | Good afternoon, Nippon Taiyo international sales. May I help you? |
| Caller | Yes. My name is Diego Menzes from PV Plastics. May I speak to Mr. Sugimoto, please? |
| Ikeda | I’m sorry. Could you repeat your name please? |
| Caller | Yes. It’s Diego Menzes. |
| Ikeda | Thank you, Mr. Menzes. One moment please. I’ll transfer your call. |
| Caller | Thank you. |

#### Asking for Names

| 🕿      | Ring-ring.                                                         |
|--------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Ikeda  | Good morning, Nippon Taiyo international sales. May I help you?    |
| Caller | Yes. May I speak to Mr. Oe, please?                                |
| Ikeda  | May I ask who’s calling, please?                                   |
| Caller | Yes. My name is Ray Carter.                                        |
| Ikeda  | And may I have the name of your company, please, Mr. Carter?       |
| Caller | Yes, I’m with Rockland Design.                                     |
| Ikeda  | Thank you, Mr. Carter. I’ll transfer your call. One moment please. |
| Caller | Thank you.                                                         |

#### The Reception Phone

In some offices, guests have to contact their counterparties by a reception phone. This is the case at Nippon Taiyo.

| 🕿 | Ring-ring. |
|----|----|
| Ikeda | Good afternoon, international sales. Ikeda speaking. How may I help you? |
| Caller | Good afternoon, Mr Ikeda. This is Randolph Singh. I am here for our two o’clock meeting. |
| Ikeda | Thank you, Mr. Singh. Please wait a moment. I’ll be right with you. |
| Caller | Thank you. |

## 2. Making an Appointment

#### Calling a Client

Takashi Ikeda calls Amanda Jones at Atlas Construction for the first time to arrange a meeting.

| 🕿 | Ring-ring. |
|----|----|
| Jones | Good morning. Atlas Construction. Amanda Jones speaking. May I help you? |
| Ikeda | Good morning, Ms Jones. My name is Takashi Ikeda. I’m from Nippon Taiyo’s sales department. |
| Jones | Yes, Mr. Ikeda. What can I do for you? |
| Ikeda | I’m Nippon Taiyo’s new sales representative for Atlas Construction. I would like to meet you to introduce myself. Could we meet sometime this week? |
| Jones | Let me look at my schedule. One moment please… I can meet you this Thursday at two o’clock. Is that convenient for you? |
| Ikeda | Yes, this Thursday at two would be fine. Thank you very much, Ms Jones. |
| Jones | Thank you, Mr. Ikeda. I’ll see you then. |
| Ikeda | Thank you again, Ms Jones. Good-bye. |
| Jones | Good-bye. |

#### Calling a Vendor

Miwa Tani in Nippon Taiyo’s purchasing department calls a vendor to arrange a meeting.

| 🕿 | Ring-ring. |
|----|----|
| Wong | Good morning. Wade Materials. Anita Wong speaking. May I help you? |
| Tani | Good morning Ms Wong. This is Miwa Tani at Nippon Taiyo. |
| Wong | Good morning Ms Tani. What can I do for you? |
| Tani | I’d like to arrange a meeting to discuss our current order for plastic sheeting. I’d like to meet tomorrow if possible. |
| Wong | Tomorrow? I’m very sorry but I’ll be away on business tomorrow. Could we make it Thursday? |
| Tani | I’m sorry no. Thursday’s not good for me. How about Friday afternoon sometime between 2 and 4? |
| Wong | Would three o’clock be alright? |
| Tani | Yes, three this Friday is alright. |
| Wong | Thank you, Ms Tani. I’ll be at your office at three this Friday. |
| Tani | Thank you Ms Wong. See you then. Good-bye. |
| Wong | Thank you for calling Ms Tani. Good-bye. |
