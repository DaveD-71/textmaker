## Who is this for?

This textbook has been prepared for employees who have the opportunity to use English in their job or will have the opportunity in the near future.

The textbook introduces four essential business communication skills.

1.  Using business **protocol**

2.  Making **phone calls**

3.  Business **presentations**

4.  Writing business **emails**

In addition, **self-study** goal setting and action planning take the student beyond the textbook and prepare them for effective study after the course ends.
