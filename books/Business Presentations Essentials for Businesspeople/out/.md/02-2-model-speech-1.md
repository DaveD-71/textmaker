# 2: Model Speech 1

- **Model Speech**

> Below is a model self-introductory speech. It is very short however, it follows the basic speech structure of introduction, body, and conclusion. Read the speech carefully.
>
> Background: Hiroshi Tanaka works for The Nippon Taiyo Bank, a large bank based in Tokyo with branches all over Japan. Below is the self-introductory speech he gave at a training program.
