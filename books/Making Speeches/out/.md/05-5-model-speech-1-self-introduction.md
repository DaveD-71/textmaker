# 5 Model Speech 1: Self-Introduction

- **Self-Introduction Speech**

> At some business meetings, it is necessary to give a short self-introductory speech.

- Self-introductions should be short and simple– about 1 minute.

- Self-introductions are usually informal but they follow the same structure and organization as longer presentations: Introduction, Body, and Conclusion.

> **Note:** When you introduce your work, do not say, “I’m in charge of…” unless you are the boss. If you are a staff member, say, “I take care of…”

- **Exercise 1: Practice**

  1.  Read the model self-introduction aloud.

  2.  Study the structure and language of the model.

- **Exercise 2: Prepare, Practice, and Present**

\[\[PAGEBREAK\]\]

2.  Practice your speech out loud, and then present it to the class.

- **Exercise 3: Expand, Practice, and Present**

  1.  Expand your short introduction by speaking about 3 points in detail.

> For example:

- Why I joined my company.

- The work I have done and the work I am currently doing.

- What I have learned from my work.

- The work I have enjoyed the most and why.

- What I would like to do in the future and why.
