# 1 Q&A About Speeches 

<img src="assets/media/image4.png" style="width:1.34375in;height:1.46875in" />

- **Question 1 What is the purpose of a speech?**

**Answer The purpose of a speech is to create a new understanding**.

To deliver a message and create action, a speech must -

- Transfer information and ideas from the speaker to the audience.

- Create understanding.

- Persuade.

<!-- -->

- **Question 2 Who decides the information and message?**

**Answer** The speaker must decide the information and message.

- The speaker must clearly explain the information and the message.

- The audience will not guess what the speaker wants to say.

<!-- -->

- **Question 3 Are all speeches the same?**

**Answer** **Yes and No.**

**No, speeches have different purposes and different audiences -**

- Sales presentations

- Technical and scientific reports

- Project or performance reports

- Training and informational presentations

- Policy proposals

**Yes. Despite the different purposes, all speeches are essentially the same.**

- All speeches are similar in their basic structure and purpose: to create understanding, agreement, and action.
