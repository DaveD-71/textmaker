- **Notes About Flag Expressions**

  1.  **Counting Vs Transitions**

> In the introduction and conclusion you can count your main points with *First*, *second*, *third*; or *First*, *then*, *next*, *finally*.
>
> However, in the body you <u>cannot</u> introduce your main points with these expressions.
>
> In the body, introduce each point with transition expressions such as -

- *Let me begin with my first point.*

- *Now, I’ll move on to my second point.*

- *Now, I will tell you my third point.*

  1.  ***At first***

> Do not use *at first* to count or to introduce your first point.

- *At first* is a different grammatical structure from counting: first, second, third.

- *At first* introduces a condition that is going to change.

- For example: *When I went to Canada, <u>at first</u> it was very difficult for me to speak English, <u>however</u>, after awhile it became much easier for me.*

  1.  **Avoid using *Let’s***

> *Let’s* is suitable only for informal proposals such as -

- *<u>Let’s</u> get started.*

- *<u>Let’s take</u> a short break.*

> *Let’s* <u>should not</u> be used for formal proposals and conclusions. \[\[LINEBREAK\]\]For example-

- *<u>Let’s</u> improve our products and advertising.* is <u>not appropriate.</u>

- *<u>I think we need</u> to improve our products and advertising.* is appropriate.
