1.  **Expressions of Order (1, 2, 3)**

Counting and using numbers gives order to your speech.

- I have three points: First,…. Second, … And third, …

- Firstly, secondly, thirdly (less common but also acceptable.)

> Note: Do not say “At first”. It is not correct for counting.

- Let me begin with my first point.

- First, I will tell you about …. Then, I will tell you about… And finally, I will tell you about…

- My first point is about…

- I would like to begin by telling you about…

- My second point is about …

- Next, I will tell you about …

- I will move on to my second point.

- Now, let’s look at my second point.

- My third point is about …

- My last point is about …

- Finally, let me tell you about …

- This brings me to my final point …

- I’ll move on to…

- Let me move on to…

- Let me continue with…

> **Note** – How to use “Then”
>
> Use *then* in your introduction to introduce what you are going to talk about.
>
> For example – *<u>First</u> I will talk about … <u>Then</u> I will talk about…*
>
> Do <u>not</u> use *then* in your body to introduce your next point.
>
> Say, *Now, I will talk about… / Next, I will talk about….*

2.  **Explaining your ideas**

- Let me give you an example.

- The reason I think this is …

- I think …

- I think …for the following reasons.

- The main reason is that…

3.  **Connecting and contrasting ideas**

- Therefore

- As a result

- So

- Then

- because (Never begin a sentence with *because*.)

- but (Never begin a sentence with *but*. Use *however*.)

- However,

- On the other hand

- In the same way

- Next

- After that

- Before that

4.  **Conclusion**

- In conclusion, let me review my main points.

- Today, I have talked about…

- I told you about …

- I explained…

- I described…

- I think that…(your message)

5.  **Closing**

- If you have any questions, I will be happy to answer them.

- Now, I’ll be happy to answer your questions.

- Thank you.

- Thank you very much.

- Thank you for your attention.

> Note: Don’t say “That’s all.” It is not appropriate for formal speeches.

6.  **Asking Questions**

- I have a question.

- Could I ask a question?

- I’d like to ask you about…

- Could you tell me more about…?

> Note: Don’t say “May I have a question.” It is incorrect.

7.  **Clarifying**

- I think you said…

- Did you say…?

- Mr. Johnson, you said… Do you mean…?

- I'm sorry, you said something about… but I didn't understand it.

- I'm sorry, I didn't understand what you said about …

- You said something about … could you explain that?

- Could you say that again, please?

- Could you repeat what you said about …, please?

- Could you clarify what you meant when you said…?

- When you said… did you mean… or …?

- I just want to be sure I understand you correctly.

- Is that correct?

- Is that right?

8.  **Responding to Questions**

- That’s a very good question.

- I'm glad you asked that.

- That's a difficult question.

- That's a tough question.

- Let's see. I think that …

- Let me think about that. … I think that…

- Yes. I said …

- Yes, that's what I meant.

- I think you misunderstood me. I meant …

- I'm sorry. Could you repeat your question, please?

> **<u>Notes & Answers</u>**
>
> **Page 5: Messages**
>
> **Option 1:** *These trends show that the market situation is difficult for our company.*
>
> **Comment: This is not an effective message.**
>
> It is simply a statement of fact. There is no analysis and no proposed action.
>
> The audience must guess what the speaker is thinking.
>
> **Option 2:** *The market is difficult for our company so we will have to think about it.*
>
> **Comment: This is not an effective message.**
>
> The suggestion to think about the situation is not followed by a proposal for action.
>
> The audience must guess what the speaker wants.
>
> **Option 3:** *Looking at the recent overseas market trends we can see that the future will be very difficult for our company. Therefore, I think we must improve our products and advertising. If we do this, I think our company will have a successful future.*
>
> **Comment: This is a good message.**
>
> It has a review and some analysis of the facts.
>
> This is followed by a clear proposal for action.
