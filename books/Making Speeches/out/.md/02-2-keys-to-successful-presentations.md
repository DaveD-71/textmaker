<td><strong>Purpose &amp; Content</strong></td>
<td><p>A successful speech needs a clear purpose and good content.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>You must have a reason for speaking and a clear message you want your audience to understand.</p></li>
<li><p>Your content should include information, analysis, and a clear message.</p></li>
</ul></td>

<td><p>The next thing you need is organization. Your ideas need to be well organized so your listeners will understand you.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Following the speech structure will make your speech easy to understand.</p></li>
</ul></td>

<td><strong>Language</strong></td>
<td><p>Expressions for speeches are called Flag Expressions.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Flag expressions help your listeners follow the structure of your speech.</p></li>
<li><p>Flag expressions tell your listeners what you are going to say.</p></li>
</ul></td>

> Practice • Memorize • Use
>
> When you know and use the keys to successful presentations
>
> you can concentrate on the most important parts of your speech:
>
> your content and message.
>
> *The more English you memorize, the more you can use.*
>
> *The more English you can use, the easier it is to communicate.*
