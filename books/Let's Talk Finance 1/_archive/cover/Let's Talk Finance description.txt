'Let's Talk Finance' introduces 20 finance-related topics for discussion. The topics cover cryptocurrency regulation, green finance initiatives, central bank digital currencies, data privacy and protection, and many more. The topics are relevant for the 21st century. Each topic comes with a half-page summary of the issue and a list of 5 questions. Prepared answers for each question support the teacher and the student and help guide them through the topic. This is an advanced level resource.
