## 2. Green Finance Initiatives

### Reading

Green finance and sustainable investing focus on directing capital towards environmentally sustainable projects, businesses, and initiatives that address climate change, environmental degradation, and social challenges. As concerns about climate change and sustainability grow, investors are increasingly seeking opportunities to align their investments with environmental, social, and governance (ESG) criteria.

Green finance encompasses a range of financial instruments and products, including green bonds, sustainable funds, impact investments, and ESG-screened portfolios, that promote sustainable development and responsible investing practices. Governments, regulators, and financial institutions play a key role in promoting green finance through policy interventions, incentives, and disclosure requirements that encourage transparency, accountability, and integration of ESG factors into investment decisions. Moreover, international initiatives such as the Paris Agreement and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) provide frameworks for mobilizing private capital towards climate-resilient infrastructure, renewable energy projects, and sustainable agriculture.

By integrating environmental and social considerations into financial decision-making, green finance and sustainable investing contribute to long-term value creation, risk mitigation, and positive societal impact.
