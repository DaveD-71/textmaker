## 15. Wealth Inequality & Redistribution Policies

### Reading

Wealth inequality and redistribution policies address disparities in wealth distribution among individuals and households through taxation, social welfare programs, and wealth redistribution mechanisms. Rising wealth inequality has become a pressing social and economic issue, with a small fraction of the population holding a disproportionate share of wealth and income.

Wealth concentration can undermine social cohesion, limit economic opportunities, and exacerbate socio-economic inequalities. Governments implement progressive taxation systems, estate taxes, and wealth transfer taxes to redistribute wealth from high-income individuals and wealthy households to fund public services, social welfare programs, and poverty reduction initiatives. Moreover, targeted social assistance programs, such as universal basic income (UBI), means-tested benefits, and asset-building initiatives, aim to provide financial support to vulnerable populations and promote social mobility.

By reducing wealth inequality, promoting inclusive growth, and ensuring equitable access to opportunities and resources, redistribution policies contribute to social stability, economic prosperity, and human development.

1.  What are the drivers of wealth inequality, including factors such as intergenerational wealth transfers, income disparities, and differential access to education and opportunities?

2.  How effective are progressive taxation systems, estate taxes, and wealth transfer taxes in reducing wealth concentration and promoting social mobility?

3.  What are the trade-offs between wealth redistribution policies aimed at reducing inequality and potential adverse effects on economic incentives, investment, and innovation?

4.  How can governments design targeted social welfare programs, such as universal basic income (UBI) schemes and means-tested benefits, to provide a safety net for the most vulnerable while minimizing welfare dependency?

5.  What role can philanthropy, impact investing, and corporate social responsibility initiatives play in addressing social inequalities and promoting sustainable development goals?
