# Let's Talk Finance (Book A) — Project Plan

## 1. Purpose and scope

Produce a reusable **reading-and-discussion resource** on 20 current issues in finance and the financial system. Each topic: one page of reading, one page of questions. It develops professional/academic English through substantial finance content and generates genuine discussion.

The existing `Let's Talk Finance.docx` (now in `../_archive/`) was a ChatGPT first draft: no citations, no vocabulary system, no level control, two topics with the wrong reading text, one malformed sentence, a broken glossary. **All 20 topics are rewritten from scratch here.** The 20 subjects are kept; the prose is not.

- **Written for Japanese learners.** The reader is an educated adult based in Japan. Each topic is framed the way this reader meets it, using the Japanese financial baseline (deflation and the "lost decades", the Bank of Japan's 2% target and 2024 exit from negative rates, the weak yen, early crypto-exchange rules after Mt. Gox/Coincheck, ageing and pensions, high cash savings, TSE governance reform, etc.). See `house-style.md` §1a — this is the single most important calibration point and was got wrong in the first prototype pass.
- **Company-neutral.** No topic is built around one company. Real company examples rotate across sectors and regions.
- **International core.** Every topic also explains how the issue looks outside Japan, with at least one clearly labelled non-Japan example; label country-specific rules as country-specific.
- **Accurate scope.** Present finance and regulation as they are, with genuine trade-offs — not "every problem has one right answer."

## 2. Target learner and level

- Educated adult **based in Japan, primarily a Japanese first-language speaker**, interested in finance and the economy for professional or general reasons; not necessarily a specialist. See `house-style.md` §1a for the Japanese-vantage-point framing rule.
- **CEFR B1+/B2** — see `../../_lets-talk-finance-shared/house-style.md` §1 for what this means sentence-by-sentence (shorter sentences, concrete before abstract, define terms on first use, active voice, 3–5-sentence paragraphs). The specialist finance terms a topic needs stay in; the sentences around them carry the load.
- Discussion questions require the learner to explain, compare, evaluate and justify — with simpler stems than the IR resource, and one guaranteed task-style question per topic.

## 3. Scale and format

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Topics | 20, in 4 Parts |
| Reading length | ~440–475 words; must fill one A4 page as a 2-column article under the Goal block, no overflow (`house-style.md` §5) |
| Topic length | 2 A4 pages (page 1 Reading, page 2 questions) |
| Citations | inline `[N]` → numbered Source Notes with live, public URLs; ≥2 factual anchors per Reading |
| Core resource | ~44 content pages + front matter + glossary |
| Later expansion | vocabulary / listening / writing activities can be added without touching the core readings |

## 5. Standard topic format

Six subsections, numbered under the topic (`X.Y.1.` … `X.Y.6.`). Full spec in `../../_lets-talk-finance-shared/house-style.md` §2:

1. **Goal** — one sentence; copied verbatim to the teacher answer book `**Aim:**` line.
2. **Reading** — the one-page 2-column article.
3. **Vocabulary Focus** — New terms (4–6) / Recycled terms (3–5, `none yet` for 1.1).
4. **Reading Questions** — exactly 5, answerable from the text.
5. **Discussion Questions** — exactly 5, ≥1 task-style, no confidential disclosure.
6. **Source Notes** — numbered, matching `[N]`, publisher + title + live URL.

## 6. Editorial principles

- Company-neutral; real examples rotate across sectors and regions (`house-style.md` §6).
- Every factual anchor cited to a reachable primary/established source; time-sensitive claims dated (`house-style.md` §3).
- Currency/number style per `house-style.md` §3; jurisdiction labels per §4, applied identically to the same rule everywhere it appears.
- Vary openings and article shapes across the 20 topics (`house-style.md` §6) — assign a shape per topic before drafting each batch.
- Balanced: present real trade-offs, not a single correct answer.

## 8. Quality criteria (per topic)

| Check | Standard |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Financial, regulatory, market and policy statements correct and appropriately qualified. |
| Level | Readable at B1+/B2 while keeping the terminology the topic needs. |
| Japanese vantage point | The topic is framed as a Japan-based reader meets it, with the correct Japanese baseline stated where it matters (`house-style.md` §1a). |
| Company-neutrality | Not built around one company; usable across sectors. Still contains ≥1 labelled non-Japan example. |
| Sourcing | ≥1 real case, ≥2 factual anchors, every anchor cited to a live public URL. |
| Teaching value | Reading questions test understanding, not copying; discussion questions get extended reasoning. |
| Independence | Understandable with no outside reading. |
| Currency | Time-sensitive claims dated and checked. |
| Page fit | Reading fills one A4 2-column page, no overflow (Phase 7 gate). |
