# Let's Talk Finance (Book A) — Company & Geography Audit

Tracks which real companies, institutions, cases, and countries appear in which topic, to catch over-reliance on one company/region and cross-topic evidence reuse **before** it becomes rework. Maintained during Phase 4, checked in Phase 5. Mirror of the IR project's `IR article company and geography audit.md`.

The shared cross-book evidence register is in `../../_lets-talk-finance-shared/topic-ledger.md` — record every primary-evidence case there too, so Book A and Book B don't collide.
