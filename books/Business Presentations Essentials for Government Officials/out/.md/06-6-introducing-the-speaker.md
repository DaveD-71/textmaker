# 6: Introducing the Speaker

- **Important expressions for introducing a speaker.**

**Introduction**

- Good afternoon, everyone.

- It’s my pleasure to introduce our first (next / last) speaker.

- Her (His) name is Mio Tanaka.

- Please give her (him) a warm welcome.

- Ms Tanaka, if you please.

**After the speech – Q&A**

- Thank you very much, Ms Tanaka.

- Now, we have time for questions.

- Yes, Mr. Oe?

- Are there any more questions?

- We have time for one more question.

- I’m sorry that’s all the time we have for questions.

**After the Q&A – close**

- Thank you everyone for your questions.

- Thank you very much, Ms Tanaka. That was a very interesting talk.

- Please give Ms Tanaka a big hand. (applause)

- And now, I’d like to ask our next chairperson to take over. Mr. Sueda, if you please.
