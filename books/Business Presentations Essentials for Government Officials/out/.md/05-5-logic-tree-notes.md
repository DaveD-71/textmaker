# 5: Logic Tree Notes

- **What is a Logic Tree?**

> When you make notes for your speech, use a logic tree.
>
> A Logic Tree is like a chart. It is an easy way to organize and remember your ideas because it has a place for each part of your speech: the theme, main points, details for each point, and your message.

- **Why is your speech like a tree?**

> Can you name the parts of the tree?

- **Logic Tree Sample 1**

> Below is a Logic Tree for speech notes: the theme is on the left, followed by the main points and supporting details; finally, the message is on the right. Extra lines can be added if needed.
>
> The Logic Tree is a very useful tool. By writing your speech notes in this pattern, it is easy to organize your ideas and information. Your speech will be complete and you won’t forget anything. With your notes in a Logic Tree, it will be easy to visualize and remember your whole speech from beginning to end.
