# How This Resource Is Organized

The 20 topics in this resource are organized around four levels at which money operates — from the household, through the company, to markets and, finally, to money's role in society. Topic numbers follow a Part.Topic format: for example, Topic 3.2 is the second topic in Part 3. Each topic's six subsections are numbered under it (e.g. 3.2.1. Goal, 3.2.2. Reading).

**Part 1: Personal Finance and Household Money** (Topics 1.1 to 1.5)
How individuals and households experience inflation, debt, saving, housing and financial fraud.

**Part 2: Companies, Work and Money** (Topics 2.1 to 2.5)
How companies raise money, how work and pay are changing, and how banks can fail.

**Part 3: Markets, Risk and the Global Economy** (Topics 3.1 to 3.5)
What moves markets, commodities and currencies, and how developing economies manage risk.

**Part 4: Money, Society and the Future** (Topics 4.1 to 4.5)
How governments tax and borrow, how money supports culture and philanthropy, and where money itself is heading.
