# Let's Talk Finance 2 (Book B) — Factual Data & Sourcing Guide

How facts and citations go into a Reading. Companion to `../../_lets-talk-finance-shared/house-style.md` §3–4. Mirror of the IR project's `IR article factual data insertion guide.md`.

## What counts as a factual anchor

A number, date, named entity, percentage, monetary value, rating, or specific requirement that a reader could look up. Each Reading needs **at least two**, and at least one **real case** (a company, regulator action, market event, vote, transaction, or crisis).

## Source hierarchy (prefer higher)

1. The primary body's own page — a regulator, central bank, standard-setter, statistics agency, or the company's own IR/press release.
2. An established news outlet reporting it — Reuters, FT, Bloomberg, Nikkei, AP, WSJ, a national broadcaster.
3. (not acceptable as the only source) blogs, explainer sites, aggregators, undated pages, Wikipedia.

If only tier 3 is available, the fact does not go in.

## Citation mechanics

- Inline `[N]` immediately after the clause it supports. Multiple sources for one fact: `[3][4]`.
- Source Notes numbered to match, in order of first appearance.
- Each Source Note: `Publisher: Title, date — URL`. Keep the URL on its own line if long.
- Check every URL live while drafting. A 404 or paywall-with-no-abstract means find another source or drop the fact.
- Date every time-sensitive claim in the prose ("in 2024", "as of early 2026") so a future reviewer can re-verify.

## Currency and numbers

Per `house-style.md` §3:
- Non-USD: `JPY 45,095.3 billion`, `EUR 1,155 million`, `GBP 2.4 billion` (ISO code + space).
- USD: `US$6.395 billion` (symbol, no space).
- `95 yen per share` OK as a plain noun; `Yen 45,095.3 billion` is not.
- `3.8%`, `up 4.4%`; "percentage points" spelled out first use.

## Jurisdiction labelling

Per `house-style.md` §4. Label every country-specific rule, and label the **same rule the same way** everywhere in the book. Where a concept is international (AML/KYC, deposit insurance, bank capital rules), say so, then give one labelled national example.

## Cross-topic reuse

Before using a company case or a specific statistic as the **primary** evidence for a point, check `company-and-geography-audit.md` and `../../_lets-talk-finance-shared/topic-ledger.md`. If it is already primary evidence in another topic, pick different evidence. A recognisable company may appear in several topics with **different** facts each time — that is fine.
