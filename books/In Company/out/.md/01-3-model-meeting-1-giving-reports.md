# 3. Model Meeting 1: Giving Reports

**Model Meeting Transcript**

Read and practice the model meeting transcript below.

Take note of how the chairperson follows the meeting structure and the language that he uses.

After practicing the model meeting transcript, take turns chairing your own meetings for practice.

There will be one short meeting simulation for each student: Each student will have a turn as chairperson and as topic leader.

Topic of the meetings: Meeting experience. Briefly describe the meetings you attend:

- Who are your meetings with?

- Why do you meet and what do you discuss?

- How often are the meetings and how long are they?

- What is your usual role in the meetings?

- What meeting skills do you need to improve?

Use the report in the transcript as a platform for your own report. Notice the three-part structure: 1) Introduction; 2) Body; 3) Conclusion.

Feedback: After each meeting the other students and the instructor will offer feedback

The Model Meeting transcript has six participants:

Chair: Mr. Hosei

Topic Leader: Ms Honda
