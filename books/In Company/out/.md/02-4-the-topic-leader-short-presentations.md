# 4. The Topic Leader: Short Presentations

> <img src="assets/media/image6.jpeg" style="width:2.39375in;height:1.52708in" />

- **The Topic Leader’s presentation is a message to the audience.**

> The message should be clear and memorable.

- The presentation has information and ideas the listeners need in order to make decisions.

> The Topic Leader should ask:

- What is my message?

- What do I want my listeners learn from my presentation?

- What do I want my listeners do?

1.  **Content: Your presentation needs three elements.**

<!-- -->

1.  **Clear Explanation:** Describe the situation, issue, or proposal.

- Use the Wh-Questions - *Who, Where, What, When, Why,* & *How*.

- Support the explanation with facts, examples, numbers, names, and details.

  1.  **Analysis:** Explain what the information means.

- Why is it important?

- Explain the costs and the benefits.

- Without your analysis, the audience will not understand the importance of your information.

  1.  **Clear Message:** Your message is your clear opinion or proposal.

- Use specific words: avoid pronouns (he, she, they, it, etc.)

- Introduce your opinion or proposal with *I think…* or *I propose…* Don’t introduce a proposal with *Let’s…*

- Without a clear message, the audience will not understand the purpose of your presentation.
