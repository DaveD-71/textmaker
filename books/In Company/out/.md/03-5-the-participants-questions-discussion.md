**The role of the participants: Listen, think critically, and ask questions.**

People have different ideas. The participants need to take an active role and share their ideas. They must listen carefully, take notes, and be prepared to ask questions, make comments, and discuss the issues on the agenda.

**<u>Gathering information</u>**

Gathering information at a meeting requires good listening skills.

Look at the speaker. This is important to tell the speaker that you are listening.

Listen carefully and take notes in English.

Think about the issues.

Think of Wh-Questions you can ask.

Make notes about the things you want to ask about.

The Wh-Questions**

Questions are essential tools to systematically analyze and organize information. They are keys that open the doors to understanding.

*Wh Questions: the Keys to Understanding & Communication*

**<u>Three Important Types of Questions</u>**

**Type 1** **Questions Clarify and Confirm**

For example: *I think you said… Is that correct?*

**Type 2 Questions Get More Information**

For example: *Could you tell me more about…?*

**Type 3 Questions Challenge (or test) Ideas**

Testing ideas and information is very important before decisions are made.

For example: *What do you think about…?*
