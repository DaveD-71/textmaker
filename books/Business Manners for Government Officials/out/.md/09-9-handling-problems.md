# 9 Handling Problems

Explaining changes & delays and apologizing

> <u>Situation: On the phone</u>
>
> Tomoya Ishii is on a research trip in Europe. He has an appointment with Alicia Phan at the European Industrial Safety Council. He has to change the appointment.

1.  Changing an appointment

> Phone Ring-ring.
>
> Phan European Safety Council. May I help you?
>
> Ishii Yes, could I speak with Alicia Phan, please?
>
> Phan Yes, speaking.
>
> Ishii Good afternoon, Ms Phan. This is Tomoya Ishii from ISRA.
>
> Phan Yes Mr. Ishii. What can I do for you?
>
> Ishii I’m calling about our meeting tomorrow at 2:30.
>
> Phan Yes?
>
> Ishii I’m very sorry, but I’m not going to be able to make it at that time. I’ve come down with a bad cold and I shouldn’t be meeting people.
>
> Phan I understand. I’m sorry to hear you’re not feeling well. When would you be able to meet?
>
> Ishii I should be better in a day or two. Could we meet on Friday?
>
> Phan One moment, please. I need to check my calendar. Yes, I’m available on Friday.
>
> Ishii Would 10:30 be convenient for you?
>
> Phan Yes, Friday morning at 10:30 would be fine.
>
> Ishii Good. I’ll see you Friday at 10:30. I’m very sorry for the inconvenience, Ms Phan.
>
> Phan Not at all, Mr. Ishii. I hope you’re feeling better soon.
>
> Ishii Thank you Ms Phan. Good-bye.
>
> Phan Thank you for calling. Good-bye.
>

2.  Explaining and apologizing for a problem

> Phone Ring-ring.
>
> Staff European Safety Council. May I help you?
>
> Ishii Yes. This is Tomoya Ishii from the Japan Industrial Safety Research Agency. May I speak to Alicia Phan, please?
>
> Staff Yes, just a moment please.
>
> Phan Alicia Phan speaking.
>
> Ishii Hello, Ms Phan. This is Tomoya Ishii from ISRA.
>
> Phan Yes, Mr. Ishii, what can I do for you?
>
> Ishii I’m calling about the extra copies of the report.
>
> Phan Yes?
>
> Ishii I’m very sorry but there’s been a delay at the printing company and they can’t be sent until next week.
>
> Phan Oh I see. I was hoping to receive them in time for a meeting early next week.
>
> Ishii I understand that. I’m sorry for the delay. The report is on the ISRA website so you could download a PDF if you like.
>
> Phan Yes, of course. I’ll do that.
>
> Ishii Good. And we’ll send the reports as soon as they are available.
>
> Phan That’s good. Thank you very much for calling, Mr. Ishii.
>
> Ishii Thank you for your understanding, Ms Phan. I’m very sorry for the delay. Good-bye.
>
> Phan Good-bye Mr. Ishii.
